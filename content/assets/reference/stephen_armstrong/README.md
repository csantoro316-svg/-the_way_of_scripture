# Bible Teaching Reference PDFs

The owner supplied this Verse By Verse Ministry International collection on 2026-10-18: **36 PDFs covering 37 books**, totaling **12,367 PDF pages**. These are internal research sources.

## Organization

- `old-testament/`: 13 PDFs covering 13 books.
- `new-testament/`: 23 PDFs covering 24 books.
- Names use each book's two-digit position in the site's 66-book Bible order and its plain name; gaps indicate books absent from this collection.
- Years and redundant download labels are omitted. 2 John and 3 John remain together in `new-testament/63-2-john-and-3-john.pdf`.
- [catalog.json](catalog.json) preserves original names, current paths, books, page counts, sizes, and SHA-256 checksums.

## Research use

Follow the [Passage Editorial Standard](../../../../documentation/architecture/PASSAGE_EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md). Lessons are a baseline, not consensus or an authorized site position. Verify claims independently, explain them in original plain-language prose, and keep teacher/ministry names off public passage pages. Do not place these PDFs in `apps/web` or reproduce their teaching outlines.

The collection retains its owner-supplied folder name. Individual course authorship must be checked rather than inferred from that folder: 2 Samuel explicitly identifies Stephen Armstrong as the original teacher and Wesley Livingston from chapter 20 onward; 2 Peter explicitly identifies its speaker as Pastor Greg Driver. These are internal provenance observations from the PDFs.

Every PDF opened with `pdfinfo`; full text was extracted with `pdftotext`, and opening/closing lesson headings were inspected for book identity. This inventory does not establish exhaustive verse coverage or independently verify teaching claims. Older PDFs, particularly Luke, have imperfect text extraction; consult the PDF visually when text is unclear.

Genesis is unchanged at 941 pages, SHA-256 `2c2ebfe3440d4c5aec4f6881dcc2e02751c0401383172e66bd0eb3a461c7c02d`. The first reviewed passage batch consulted lessons 1A–1D, combined PDF pages 1–30. Publisher course: https://versebyverseministry.org/bible-studies/genesis-2011.
