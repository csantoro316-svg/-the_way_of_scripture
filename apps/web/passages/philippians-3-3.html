Passage

Philippians 3:3

What does worship by God’s Spirit mean here?

Berean Standard Bible

3 For it is we who are the circumcision, we who worship by the Spirit of God, who glory in Christ Jesus, and who put no confidence in the flesh—

What is the context?

Paul is warning against teachers who would turn circumcision and outward standing into a basis for confidence. He identifies his own community through worship by the Spirit, glorying in Christ and no confidence in the flesh.

The word translated worship is latreuō (lah-TREW-oh). Thayer’s lexicon explains religious service and worship and identifies God’s Spirit as prompting that service here. The verse describes who these people are and whom they trust.

The surrounding passage matters: Paul goes on to name his own inherited and religious credentials, then explain why knowing Christ has become his greater gain. Read Philippians 3:1–6 for the immediate contrast.

How is this passage commonly understood?

Christians commonly understand this as describing God’s people through Spirit-led worship and confidence in Christ. Wesley’s note emphasizes inward holiness, and the USCCB note connects the description with belonging to God and the promise to Abraham. The shared emphasis reaches into the heart and the source of a person’s confidence.

Where does this fit in God's plan?

Deuteronomy 30:6 describes God circumcising His people’s hearts so they will love Him. Romans 2:28–29 also describes circumcision of the heart by the Spirit.

This supported connection helps place the verse within the larger scriptural concern for God’s people to love and serve Him from the heart. Paul directs the readers’ confidence to Christ and the work of God’s Spirit.

What is our position?

On verse 3, our study Worship as the Early Church Knew It understands worship by the Spirit as sacred service to God expressed in daily obedience. The study emphasizes a life led by His Spirit rather than reading this verse as an instruction about music. Its focus is the service of people whose confidence is in Christ.
