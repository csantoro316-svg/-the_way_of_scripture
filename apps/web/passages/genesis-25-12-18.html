Passage

Genesis 25:12-18

The Descendants of Ishmael

Berean Standard Bible

12 This is the account of Abraham’s son Ishmael, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah’s maidservant, bore to Abraham. 13 These are the names of the sons of Ishmael in the order of their birth: Nebaioth the firstborn of Ishmael, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, 14 Mishma, Dumah, Massa, 15 Hadad, Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah.

16 These were the sons of Ishmael, and these were their names by their villages and encampments—twelve princes of their tribes. 17 Ishmael lived a total of 137 years. Then he breathed his last and died, and was gathered to his people.

18 Ishmael’s descendants settled from Havilah to Shur, which is near the border of Egypt as you go toward Asshur. And they lived in hostility toward all their brothers.

What is the context?

Genesis briefly follows Ishmael's family before returning to Isaac. It identifies Ishmael as Abraham's son through Hagar, names his twelve sons, and describes them as princes of their tribes. Their villages and encampments show a family that has grown into distinct communities.

The twelve leaders recall God's promise in Genesis 17:20, where He said He would bless Ishmael, multiply his descendants, and make him the father of twelve rulers. That promise is remembered even though the covenant line is traced through Isaac. Being outside that particular line does not mean being outside every kindness God has shown.

Ishmael dies at 137. The account describes his descendants' territory in relation to Egypt and Asshur and, in the BSB, their hostility toward their brothers. It closes this branch of the family history without requiring us to assign the character of these communities to every later person who claims descent from Ishmael.

How is this passage commonly understood?

A common Christian reading sees the twelve princes as fulfillment of God's promise to Ishmael, while the main story continues through Isaac. Wesley's notes on Genesis 25:11, 17 treat Ishmael's growing family and long life as signs of God's care for him.
