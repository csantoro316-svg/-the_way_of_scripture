Passage

Ezekiel 48:1-7

Which tribes receive the northern portions?

Berean Standard Bible

1 “Now these are the names of the tribes: At the northern frontier, Dan will have one portion bordering the road of Hethlon to Lebo-hamath and running on to Hazar-enan on the border of Damascus with Hamath to the north, and extending from the east side to the west side. 2 Asher will have one portion bordering the territory of Dan from east to west. 3 Naphtali will have one portion bordering the territory of Asher from east to west. 4 Manasseh will have one portion bordering the territory of Naphtali from east to west. 5 Ephraim will have one portion bordering the territory of Manasseh from east to west. 6 Reuben will have one portion bordering the territory of Ephraim from east to west. 7 Judah will have one portion bordering the territory of Reuben from east to west.

What is the context?

The northern allotments are named in order: Dan, Asher, Naphtali, Manasseh, Ephraim, Reuben and Judah. Each portion runs from east to west beside the next.

Manasseh and Ephraim receive separate portions in keeping with Joseph’s double share in 47:13. Genesis 48:3–7 records Jacob receiving Joseph’s two sons as his own, and First Chronicles 5:1–2 distinguishes Joseph’s birthright from the rulership associated with Judah.

The Lord does give the names and arrangement of the tribes. The account moves from the outer boundaries to the people receiving their places within them.

How is this passage commonly understood?

Cambridge reads these as the tribes north of the holy district, beginning with Dan at the northern edge. Henry understands the whole chapter as the ordered portions belonging to the tribes, while also drawing Christian applications from the vision.

The tribal list is part of the restoration picture: the people are assigned an inheritance rather than remaining scattered without a place.
