Passage

Daniel 12:8-10

What is Daniel told when he still does not understand?

Berean Standard Bible

8 I heard, but I did not understand. So I asked, “My lord, what will be the outcome of these things?” 9 “Go on your way, Daniel,” he replied, “for the words are closed up and sealed until the time of the end. 10 Many will be purified, made spotless, and refined, but the wicked will continue to act wickedly. None of the wicked will understand, but the wise will understand.

What is the context?

Daniel hears the answer but does not understand it. He asks what the outcome will be and is told to go on his way: the words remain closed and sealed until the time of the end.

The reply does describe the different responses of people living through the testing. Many will be purified and refined, while the wicked continue in wickedness. The wise will understand.

The passage distinguishes receiving a true message from having immediate understanding of every part. Daniel is called to trust what has been given. The wisdom in view is tied to faithfulness through testing, rather than simply possessing more information than others.

How is this passage commonly understood?

Wesley emphasizes that the events will bring clearer understanding. Cambridge connects the wise with the faithful teachers already described in chapter 11, whose insight shows in the course they choose under pressure. Both connect understanding with a faithful response to God.
