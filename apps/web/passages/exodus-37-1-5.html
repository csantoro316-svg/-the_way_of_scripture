Passage

Exodus 37:1-5

Why does the furniture begin with the ark?

Berean Standard Bible

1 Bezalel went on to construct the ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long, a cubit and a half wide, and a cubit and a half high. 2 He overlaid it with pure gold, both inside and out, and made a gold molding around it. 3 And he cast four gold rings for its four feet, two rings on one side and two on the other. 4 Then he made poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold. 5 He inserted the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark in order to carry it.

What is the context?

With the dwelling’s structure made, the account turns to its furnishings. Bezalel makes the ark from acacia wood, overlays it with gold inside and out, and provides rings and gold-covered poles for carrying it. This is the chest God instructed Moses to make for the covenant testimony in Exodus 25:10–16.

The ark belongs in the innermost room, behind the veil. The next passage describes its golden cover and cherubim. Keeping the ark and its cover together helps us understand their purpose: the testimony of God’s covenant is kept where He promises to meet with His people.

How is this passage commonly understood?

Calvin reads the close match between the command and the finished ark as a testimony to faithful obedience. Gill understands Bezalel’s name as identifying the craftsman directing the work, without requiring the conclusion that he made every part alone.

Where does this fit in God's plan?

Hebrews 9:1–5, 23–28 recalls the ark within the earthly sanctuary, then directs attention to Christ entering heaven itself to appear before God on our behalf. The carefully made furnishings belong to an earlier pattern of worship that points beyond itself to His completed work.

The covenant testimony also belongs within God’s larger purpose for His people. Hebrews 8:8–12 describes the new covenant in terms of God’s laws written in minds and hearts, knowing Him, and receiving forgiveness. God’s aim reaches into the people themselves: His word shapes a people who belong to Him.
