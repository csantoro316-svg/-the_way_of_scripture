Passage

2 Corinthians 7:5-7

How did God bring comfort to Paul in Macedonia?

Berean Standard Bible

5 For when we arrived in Macedonia, our bodies had no rest, but we were pressed from every direction—conflicts on the outside, fears within. 6 But God, who comforts the downcast, comforted us by the arrival of Titus, 7 and not only by his arrival, but also by the comfort he had received from you. He told us about your longing, your mourning, and your zeal for me, so that I rejoiced all the more.

What is the context?

Paul resumes the travel account interrupted after 2 Corinthians 2:12–13. Arriving in Macedonia had not brought immediate relief. There were conflicts around him and fears within.

The comfort came through the arrival of Titus. Paul identifies God as the one who comforts the downcast and recognizes His care in this meeting. Titus also brings the comfort he received from the Corinthians themselves.

Their longing, mourning and zeal for Paul have made an impression on Titus, and hearing that report increases Paul’s joy. Comfort travels through the relationship: the congregation refreshes Titus, and his presence and news refresh Paul.

The passage gives a concrete account of God’s care through another person. It does not require a secret sign or deny that a faithful servant can experience fear and discouragement.

How is this passage commonly understood?

Christians commonly understand the arrival and report of Titus as the way God comforted Paul in this situation. Wesley’s notes on verses 5–7 connect the fears with concern for the church and its response. The USCCB notes on verses 5–7 likewise read the meeting as the resolution of the waiting described in chapter 2.

Where does this fit in God's plan?

Romans 12:4–5 and 15 describes believers as members of one body and calls them to rejoice with those who rejoice and weep with those who weep.

The meeting here gives a concrete example of that shared life. God’s purpose includes a people who carry care and encouragement to one another. Paul’s experience shows why another person’s presence and honest news can matter within that work.
