Passage

Genesis 26:34-35

Esau’s Wives

Berean Standard Bible

34 When Esau was forty years old, he took as his wives Judith daughter of Beeri the Hittite and Basemath daughter of Elon the Hittite. 35 And they brought grief to Isaac and Rebekah.

What is the context?

At forty, Esau marries two Hittite women, Judith and Basemath. Genesis says that they bring grief to Isaac and Rebekah, without describing particular conversations or incidents. The short notice interrupts the account of Isaac's outward prosperity with trouble inside his family.

The marriages also prepare for what follows. Abraham had sought a wife for Isaac from his relatives rather than the Canaanites in chapter 24. Rebekah will raise her distress over the Hittite women again in Genesis 27:46, and Isaac will send Jacob away to find a wife among her family. Esau's marriages are therefore part of the setting for Jacob's departure.

How is this passage commonly understood?

A common Christian reading sees Esau's marriages as a source of family grief and a contrast with the concern to preserve the family's calling when choosing a spouse. Wesley's Genesis 26 overview and note on verse 34 make that connection.
