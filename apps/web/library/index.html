Foundations: A Guided Study

Work through a connected series of studies, beginning with why we return to Scripture's words and context, then how to examine every teaching against the text. Continue through the gospel, discipleship, prayer, and life together as Christ’s people. Each study builds on what came before. Take your time, examine the passages, and continue when you’re ready.
