## Acts 8:1–8
Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): Acts 1:8 names the regions that receive the scattered witnesses; persecution does not end the purpose of their witness.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The baseline's claims that all Jews had permanently lost their opportunity, that Samaria had no city, and that all apostles stayed through disobedience are not established by Luke. Broad claims about when all New Testament books were written, legal restrictions on mourning, and a specific Roman execution window lack independent verification here. No such claims appear publicly. A word's possible seed-related derivation does not establish a parable allusion.

## Acts 8:9–25
Plan section: included, because (b)/(c): the comparison with John 4 and Luke 9 highlights Samaritan welcome and John's changed service without claiming hidden motives.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The baseline's certainty about Simon's final condition goes beyond the narrative; checked primary commentators differ. Peter's rebuke does not lexically establish an exclusive unbeliever category, and silence about Simon's reception of the Spirit proves neither conclusion. The proposed present/future meanings of part/share and a claim that word here must mean Christ were not independently verified; the actual BSB says ministry. Later legends about Simon and a claim that his request for prayer necessarily proves refusal are not used. No invented racial generalizations or claim that Luke explicitly reports tongues. The account does not say all 120 Jerusalem believers had precisely the same baptismal sequence.

## Acts 8:26–40
Plan section: included, because (a)/(b)/(c): Philip explicitly begins with Isaiah 53 to explain Jesus; Isaiah 56 is a separately labeled parallel, not a quotation Luke supplies.
Position source: Baptism.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The official's circumcision, exact status as proselyte or God-fearer, specific queen, scroll cost, and later founding of a church are not established here. No precise road identification, miraculous creation of a pool, universal ancient reading habit, or physical explanation of Philip's transport is asserted. The BSB's descendants wording is preserved rather than replacing it with a proposed meaning about explaining Jesus to His family. Verse 37 remains outside the main quotation. Physical castration is not inferred as a documented personal history. No new owner position about all baptism modes is originated.
