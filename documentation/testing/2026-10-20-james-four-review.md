## James 4:4–6

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). 2 Corinthians 11:2–4 explicitly applies faithful-marriage language to the church's devotion to Christ; this does not decide 4:5's grammar.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline confident single Spirit/yearning explanation is not consensus. Calvin reports human-spirit envy but prefers a question denying envy of God's Spirit; Wesley reads Spirit opposed to envy; USCCB documents God/Spirit longing jealously. These are attributed interpretations, not whole-denomination pigeonholes. BSB capital Spirit is preserved rather than silently lowercased or replaced. No equation of sinful human envy with God's righteous claim on His people is imposed. No exact unlocated quotation identified as definitely a particular lost text. The public page does not convert one teacher's textual doubts about verse 6 into omission; actual BSB and explicit Proverb-derived wording remain intact. Friendship with world is not a ban on compassion for nonbelievers or on created goods.

## James 4:7–10

Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). Philippians 2:5–11 explicitly calls believers to Christ's mindset; the direction of humility and exaltation is a labeled parallel with His unique lordship distinguished.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Resist means oppose; it does not lexically mean only prebattle preparation or guarantee no further temptation in a believer's lifetime. Calvin explicitly acknowledges repeated attacks while affirming God's help. Humility is not described as earning initial grace. Drawing near is not confined by lexical fiat to one meeting, prescribed duration or modern service form; the baseline's claim all gatherings only training and outside service necessarily more significant is not established here. No unverified historical exclusivity of a Levitical expression is asserted. Mourning concerns repentance, not a prohibition of joy or an invitation to diagnose every depressed person as repentant. Christ's exaltation does not prove identical authority or rank for every humble believer.

## James 4:13–17

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Proverbs 27:1 supplies the earlier wisdom concern; Luke 12:47–48 connects known will with responsibility to act.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Planning is not itself forbidden; no requirement to receive a private sign before each decision is added. The owner signs study supports guidance elsewhere but does not supply a specific position on James 4:13–17. Vapor is a transience picture, not proof the author specifically means cold-weather breath. The final verse includes failure to do known good, not merely failure to speak a formula.
