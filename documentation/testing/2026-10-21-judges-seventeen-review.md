FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline late-Judges/Philistine setting differs from Wesley/Henry's early setting. No exact date or certain chronology asserted. No certainty that Samuel wrote Judges and Ruth together or that these chapters record the first appearance of all idolatry in Israel.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Micah's name is not introduced because the baseline's assertion that it means one who is like Yahweh was not lexically checked. No claim mother's curse was Spirit-inspired prophecy. Her blessing and dedication are reported, without claiming they automatically carry God's endorsement.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Only two hundred of the eleven hundred are specified for the silversmith. Baseline calls retaining nine hundred theft from God, while Wesley allows other worship expenses; no narrated accounting for the balance establishes the accusation. Omit that verdict.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Exact image shape, a carved figure atop a cast base, high-priest-only ephod identification and precise teraphim function at this shrine are not established. BDB confirms the general word range, not every practice at Micah's house.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: A Levite is not therefore an Aaron descendant. Numbers 18 distinguishes the wider tribe from Aaron's sons; no invented genealogical qualification. No certain explanation that lost tithes caused this particular man's travels, or that Bethlehem departure is a coded rejection of Christ.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Do not infer subconscious debt psychology for all religions or condemn a modern church from this text without checking its own documents. No such comparison retained. No newly invented Cain offering command, universal postexilic absence of idolatry, or guaranteed wealth from a hired priest.
