## Per-group decisions

### Matthew5:21–26 — Anger and Reconciliation

- Plan section: included, because(c). SecondCorinthians5:17–21 describes God's reconciliation through Christ/new creation;Ephesians4:29–32 connects forgiveness in Christ with changed speech/kindness;1John3:11–18 connects hatred/murder contrast with Christ's self-giving and active love. These add the larger purpose of a reconciled people without imposing a detailed identity on every court character.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: blanket claim all six 'you heard' quotations are only written Mishnah rather than Scripture is false; actual murder/adultery commands are in Torah. God's Law already forbids inner hatred/coveting (Lev19/Ex20), so no claim Moses cared only about external conduct. Completed written Mishnah universally ruling every Jew's life, murder only sinful after a rabbinic conviction, and all Pharisees' identical motives/wealth/hypocrisy are not established by checked primary evidence. Exact assembly size23/72, exclusive execution procedures, priestly permissions and later rabbinic legal processes not independently verified here. No assertion every insult has exactly the moral gravity/punishment of mass murder, or that any hurt feeling automatically makes the other person innocent/guilty. The BSB main wording omits without-a-cause; no other reading inserted. Reconciliation before worship is the actual direction; a particular private offense, unconscious conscience mechanism and inevitable success of every reconciliation attempt are not narrated. God's being exactly the creditor/judge in every detail is a sourced application in Wesley, explicitly disputed by Gill; it is not a site conclusion. Neither earthly debt story nor God's judgment proves a compulsory purgatory account; no supporting primary formal document directly confirmed here, so such a claim left off. Historical medieval Gehenna rubbish fires not silently adopted from a lexicon/commentary as independent evidence. No invented immediate-after-death fate for unnamed people or stereotyped Catholics/Mormons/Jews. The practical appeal is kept separate from an invented way to earn salvation by settling a lawsuit.

### Matthew5:27–30 — Desire and Faithfulness of Heart

- Plan section: included, because(c). Colossians3:5–10 places rejection of evil desires within the new self/Creator's image;Romans6:12–14 directs body parts toward God in a life brought out of death;1Corinthians6:18–20 connects sexual faithfulness with redemption and honoring God in the body. No forced person-by-person typology; this is a wider redemption/renewal theme.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: right hand as an established first-century genital euphemism was not independently confirmed from a primary cultural source or the checked lexicons; not used. No substitution of another body part into the BSB. Physical injury as a means of earning salvation or curing a heart's desire is not a verified common interpretation; the checked Chrysostom/Wesley texts supply a figurative/costly-separation reading. Not all seeing, attraction, unwanted intrusive thoughts or every desire is lexically identical to the purposeful lust described; the verb also has nonsexual/positive uses. No blame transferred to the person being looked at. No assertion an outward adultery and an inner desire produce identical earthly harms or legal consequences; Jesus' inward moral seriousness is preserved. Universal claim every Pharisee refused reconciliation/repentance or every Jew relied on an identical rulebook is unverified and omitted. No speculation about exactly which sacrifice each hearer should make or a medical/psychological treatment claim. No new site position on a detailed sexual ethic or the figurative language; precise questions await developed study. The broad Gospel study link does not substitute for owner authority on the passage.
