## Flags to investigate or omit

- Alexander Severus naming the Golden Rule and engraving it in gold on palace walls: no independent ancient source checked. Omit the origin story unless actually established. The saying can be explained without it.
- The technical label inclusio is unnecessary public vocabulary. Explain the repeated Law/Prophets language and the Sermon's unity in plain words if used. Do not infer unrecorded motives or what every hearer knew.
- All Jews presumed saved by ethnicity, all Pharisees held identical beliefs, all ordinary Jews were biblically illiterate, Abraham physically rescuing every Jew from hell: unverified sweeping history; no contemporary primary evidence checked. No stereotype or blanket accusation.
- Exact narrow city gates, refuse paths, locations, and universal gate counts are unverified here. Jesus' actual wide/narrow contrast does not require that reconstruction.
- Being in the minority does not itself prove truth, nor does being popular itself prove error. The lesson eventually concedes this; keep Scripture/fruit, not a reverse popularity test, as the criterion.
- Every false teacher is necessarily unregenerate, every sentence from one is false, or any incorrect teaching makes every other word unusable: not established by the tree image alone. Do not originate a site teacher-blacklist policy. Check 1 Thessalonians 5:20–21, Matthew23:2–3, and relevant examples if addressing these questions; these exact ranges have **not been newly read in this research**.
- Miraculous claims in 7:22 must all have actually occurred and must all be demonic: the saying reports what the speakers claim, without explicitly narrating each event or naming its power. Neither authenticity nor source should be invented.
- Judgment in 7:21–23 concerns leaders only and cannot ever address individual self-examination: one teacher's restriction, not independently verified consensus. Read the actual whole paragraph and primary interpreters.
- Doing Jesus' words means only mentally accepting propositions, with no lived response, or alternatively earning salvation by works: neither should be imposed as a new site conclusion. Represent actual readings fairly and distinguish authorized positions from the assistant's synthesis.
- Precise final-judgment mechanics, saved-population ratios, universal eternal-security assumptions, all Pharisees' moral condition, and all supposed miracles' cause need actual evidence. Do not carry them forward from the PDF as facts.
