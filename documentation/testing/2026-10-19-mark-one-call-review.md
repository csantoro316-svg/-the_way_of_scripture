## Mark 1:14–15

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Romans 1 explicitly calls the promised gospel concerning the Son gospel of God and includes resurrection; Romans 14 links kingdom with righteousness/peace/joy and mutual building. The plan adds God's promises and community purpose beyond the short lexical explanation.

Position source: The Gospel According to Jesus, limited to repentance/faith/gift and changed life, not an invented timetable.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No lexical proof paradidomi inherently means handing over because of personal self-interest; no invented exact Satan strategy behind John's arrest or claim Jesus fled highly populated places for sparse Galilee. No exact elapsed time inferred from after, no denial of earlier ministry (John3:22–24). Kairos/chronos ranges are not rigidly nonoverlapping; no bowl-overflow image as pleroo's encoded meaning. No assertion the kingdom gospel is a different saving message from Paul's gospel: Romans1 uses the same gospel of God phrase concerning Jesus; stages in the narrative are distinguished without inventing rival gospels. No mandatory single millennium account. No idea faith is bare fact without trust or repentance mere emotion. No universal doctrine of compelled conversion is declared from this summary. Existing study contains historical herald and weekday wording not needed for narrow position and not imported.

## Mark 1:16–20

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Mark3 directly appoints these same men to be with Jesus and preach; Mark6 shows their later sending and repentance message. John15 explicitly describes Jesus' choosing and appointing for fruit.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No unsupported exact lake size/depth/elevation or economic/strategic motives attributed to the Father. No guarantee every fisherman was rich, highly successful or wholly uneducated; Mark only records vocation/boat/hired men. No claim rabbi recruitment was universally unheard of or all schools followed modern college admissions equivalents. No assertion the unnamed John1 disciple must be John; actual text names Andrew only in that pair. No proof their question about lodging encoded a formal legal lifelong pledge. No claimed fixed chronology of every call: Calvin treats Luke's miraculous catch as same call, while other chronologies may distinguish stages; public attributes the accounts without fixing all dates. Mark reports no catch miracle here, so it is not silently inserted. No literal need every believer leave every job or family unsupported, and no reduction of salvation to vocational sacrifice. No doctrine that fishermen themselves create spiritual life by their own power; Jesus' shaping work and their response are retained. Related study links do not authorize an unworked site position.
