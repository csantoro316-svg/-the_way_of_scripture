## Per-group decisions

### Matthew10:16–23 — Facing Opposition with Wisdom and Faith

- Plan section: included, because (c). Acts4:8–13 shows Spirit-enabled testimony to Christ's death/resurrection before rulers; Acts8:1–4 shows scattered believers continuing proclamation. Adds the saving message's spread under pressure beyond only advising wise behavior.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: wolves are not identified in the text as exclusively demons instead of the hostile people named in17–18. No proof serpent specifically means imitate Satan's intelligence or dove means God must be sinlessly mimicked in every detail. Snakes/poodles intelligence rankings, ancient shepherds' universal financial liability, all Satan's relative intelligence/power rankings, or Michael's deference proving no creature can resist Satan are unverified and omitted. Job/Isaiah do not authorize an invented personal explanation that every abuse, trauma, car problem, phone interruption, or argument is a known targeted demonic attack. No supernatural diagnosis or modern enemy chart. No travel-cover/deception rule, private missionary anecdotes, or every trick short of sin template. Wisdom/innocence stand together. No encouragement to remain in every dangerous situation; Jesus explicitly commands flight. No claim every instance of fear/emotional distress is sin, all Christians immune to attacks, or mere personal spirituality guarantees safety. No broad prohibition of study/preparation from the trial promise. The baseline's first-century apostle-dispersal account is corrected by actual Acts8:1: the apostles remain while others scatter. No universal history that the Church is always strongest under violence, all Jews hated every apostle, or every apostle's martyrdom documented. Endurance is not rewritten as a tribute/toll/earned-reward preservation paraphrase; actual Thayer senses and sentence object checked. The salvation warnings are not explained away, nor turned into a newly originated works-salvation doctrine. Confessions' actual grace/perseverance differences and possible restoration are fairly described.23 has different verified primary readings; none is silently made the site's official timetable, and no exact end date, exhaustive future travel map, or all-Jews-reached calculation is invented.

### Matthew10:24–33 — Courage Under the Father’s Care

- Plan section: included, because (c). Romans8:31–39 grounds confidence in God's gift of His Son, resurrection/intercession, and love through danger/death. Adds the saving source of care beyond a generic assurance that God notices small things.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Beelzebul's precise Lord-of-Flies etymology is not independently checked and not stated. Actual related accusation is cited without a name-origin claim. No generic rank theorem that an apprentice can never surpass a human teacher; the passage concerns sharing Jesus' treatment. No assertion every anxious reaction proves sin, that forbidding fear bans sensible self-protection, or God's care means every witness avoids injury/death. No stronger metaphysical decree about every bird's death than the actual words, no prediction of a particular person's lifespan, and no instruction to disregard danger. No claim the warning can never apply to any hearer, cannot concern salvation, or is merely about losing rewards. Confession/denial warning remains real without inventing a new assurance or final-state doctrine. No independent afterlife geography, perpetually burning garbage-dump history, or conclusion that one verb settles every dispute about eternal punishment. The shared reading identifies God's ultimate authority and care without a fabricated consensus about every mechanism. No hidden-everything chronology or assurance that every private dispute will resolve on a particular earthly date. No DVR/sport outcome analogy substituted for the actual saving work of Christ.

- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: sword is explained in the household-division setting and beside enemy-love teaching, not as a command to attack relatives or a general justification of violence. No demand to create conflict for its own sake, abandon every duty to dependents, disregard victims, or say family love has no value. Actual more-than comparison retained. No declaration every broken relationship is God's desired outcome or that bereavement/trauma costs are meaningless. Following Jesus does not require treating others' earthly suffering as trivial. No exact Roman execution customs/historical procedure used without independent evidence; the later Gospel's own cross and resurrection provide the supported context. Life-loss promise is not reduced to a guaranteed wealth/comfort arrangement or one teacher's reward schedule. Neither every inconvenience is made identical to martyrdom nor actual death risk softened away. No new site position on faith/merit, family exceptions, or the timetable of glory.
