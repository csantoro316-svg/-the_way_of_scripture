## Per-group decisions

### Matthew9:1–8 — Jesus Has Authority to Forgive

- Plan section: included, because (c). Luke24:44–49 explicitly joins Christ's death/resurrection with forgiveness proclaimed to all nations. Adds the saving mission beyond repeating that the visible healing demonstrates authority.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: no independent evidence for the baseline's never-before-used Leviticus14/first-cleansed-Jew/formal two-stage Messiah investigation, or for a known temple report causing these particular visitors to arrive. These claims remain excluded, as in chapter8. No universal reconstruction of Pharisaic salvation-by-ethnicity, inability to repent, court-like control of all forgiveness, or malicious motives of every scribe. Matthew identifies some thoughts; crowds do not prove intentional refusal to make space. No proof the building was an inner courtyard, exact distance traveled, every person present a scribe, or exact roof engineering; only Mark/Luke's actual details used. No specific lifetime disability, private disappointment, hoped-for toys, thoughts at death, or forgiveness of every bearer is narrated. Thayer does not justify the instantaneous-action grammatical shortcut. No automatic inference that this man's paralysis was caused by a particular personal sin, despite Wesley's suggestion. Technical kal-v'chomer label, translation, and historical usage were not independently checked and are unnecessary. No rule that every apparent miracle certifies all doctrine or all power necessarily from God; Matthew7 was already reviewed. Catholic and Reformed ministry descriptions stay rooted in Christ and their actual documents, without inventing a new site decision on sacramental confession or delegating independent divine power to anyone.

### Matthew9:9–13 — Mercy for Those Who Need a Savior

- Plan section: included, because (c).1Timothy1:12–17 supplies mercy reaching a former persecutor as an example for future believers; Titus3:3–7 supplies salvation by mercy, renewal by the Spirit, and eternal-life hope. Adds changed life and future rescue beyond simply repeating compassionate table fellowship.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: the baseline's Roman empire stretching to India, precise daily tax quotas/beat lash/imprisonment regime, compulsory direct Roman employment of this Galilean collector, exact border station/route, universal local citizenship rules, and all collectors' compensation arrangements were not independently verified and are omitted. No claim every tax collector extorted people, had no friends, was beyond repentance, could never testify, or could legally associate only with prostitutes. Gospel language sinners does not establish all guests were prostitutes. Levi's name does not prove Levitical ancestry; conversion name-change and its exact cause are unrecorded. No forced decision on literary authorship or speculative renaming; the named Gospel accounts are compared as they stand. No private shame, envy, years without conversation, pinching himself, exact thoughts, or household security biography invented. No universal claim Pharisees never entered the house or all rejected mercy; the actual question/response suffices. Exact reclining furniture and all ancient dining practices lack independent verification here. No partisan or modern-group targeting, risk-taking rule, or claim every visitor automatically repented/saved. The Hosea quote critiques outward sacrifice separated from faithful knowledge of God; it does not by itself establish the baseline's two competing law/faith covenants or an entirely faithless Old Testament relationship. BDB's nuanced Hosea usage retained. Irrelevant public cautions about unrecorded biography/prostitutes were moved out of prose into this evidence during the family-reader pass.

### Matthew9:14–17 — The Bridegroom and the Time to Fast

- Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). John3:27–30 supplies the directly supported bridegroom/friend/joy parallel; Luke22:14–20 and Hebrews8:6–13 place Jesus' saving death within the promised new covenant, forgiveness, and laws written on hearts. No forced item-by-item identity of cloth/wine/skins.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: exact Thursday meal date, Luke's universal chronology, required Monday/Thursday fasts and seasonal limits, Sinai ascent weekday, universal oral-law obligation, and motives of each questioner are not independently established. No claim John's disciples could only be unbelieving Pharisaic imitators, all rejected Jesus, sought replacement rabbi, or that John had already been beheaded at this encounter; Matthew does not state this, and later Gospel prison/messenger narratives require careful chronology. No present-day religious group's identity is derived. No claim every Pharisee had the same two-covenant system or the whole Mishnah already existed as a finalized document in Jesus' lifetime. No proven assignment of first answer exclusively to John's disciples and second exclusively to Pharisees. Welding this into an established exact chronology is unwarranted. No independent universal single-use wineskin, exact fermentation history/chemistry, all Jewish wedding fasting exemptions, or impossibility of joy/fasting together is established. Askos means leather container; Jesus' own burst/preservation picture supplies the immediate contrast. No hidden wine-is-Jesus-being-poured-from-law or old-patch-is-all-rabbinic-rules identity presented as text. No assertion the images alone erase every part of Mosaic law, establish one dispensational or covenant system, or guarantee bodily transformation because both objects are preserved. Wesley's readiness explanation and bishops' wider-gospel explanation are attributed fairly; neither made the only Christian view. John's3 joy is a supported parallel, not access to these questioners' minds. Future fasting and Matthew6 prevent a blanket abolition-of-fasting reading.
