Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline chronology around AD29 does not settle every date, and differs from earlier Matthew baseline assumptions; no fixed calendar is published. Numbers 4 concerns Kohathite service and does not itself establish a universal priestly minimum age or Jesus' admission to Levitical priesthood; not used as proof of His priesthood. No exact Nazareth elevation (PDF's 500 miles is plainly unusable), population of 500, one-stop-sign metaphor as ancient fact, or universal claim nothing significant came from the town. Nathanael's comment is not a census. No assured Nazareth/netzer etymology or claim Isaiah literally names Nazareth. The Greek schizō is not a Hebrew transliteration; qara word link is not presented that way. No claim Mark specifies every witness seeing/hearing the sign; John's separate testimony is attributed to John. No established all-Jewish Spirit=dove convention, mother-bird-over-eggs detail or bat kol cultural rule without independent primary evidence. Like a dove does not require the Spirit's nature to be an ordinary bird. No claim Jesus becomes divine or first receives any Spirit at baptism; source's own fuller texts retain prior Sonship. No five-reasons list copied from the teacher; the actual obedience/identification links are developed from checked sources.

## Mark 1:12–13

Plan section: included, because (a)/(b)/(c). Exodus 4 and Deuteronomy 8 sonship/testing form labeled parallel, strengthened by Jesus' Deuteronomy answers in Matthew 4. Hebrews 2 and 4 explicitly connect His real testing and sinlessness with mercy and aid for people now.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No claim Luke's ordering of temptations is conclusively chronological against Matthew. Mark does not give the exchanges, fast or animals' behavior; details from other Gospels are attributed. No exact identity/species or claim animals were harmless from Wesley alone; USCCB permits danger and creation-peace emphases, so neither becomes text's certain meaning. No universal guarantee Christians cannot be harmed by wild animals or every trial ends visibly safely. No literal all-temptations-only-from-devil rule (James also names personal desire), no suggestion God's purpose is to entice evil, no inference Spirit must overcome Jesus' sinful unwillingness from drives. No diagnosis of Satan's physical visible form or concrete-test analogy as historical information. No claimed necessary internal lust in sinless Jesus. No exact temptation-to-three-categories code from 1 John 2:16 as Scripture's exhaustive taxonomy, or universal promise knowing enough Bible eliminates every doubt. No claim Mark's Roman audience was certainly reading amid lions with a specific emotional response. Adam connection is supported elsewhere but not added through an assumed peaceful-beasts detail. Stronger explicitly supported Israel/Hebrews connections suffice.
