## Sources actually read

Actual main-text BSB Acts 12 read in full, 25 verses. Newly read BSB cross-references: Mark 10:35–40; John 21:18–19; Daniel 4:28–37. Mark names the brothers' future sharing of the cup but explicitly leaves the seats to those for whom they are prepared. No inference about John dying on Patmos or a guaranteed particular seat is made. Final quotations require fresh independent verification.

Whole Wesley XII read directly at https://wesley.nnu.edu/john-wesley/john-wesleys-notes-on-the-bible/notes-on-the-acts-of-the-apostles/ . Contains a guardian-angel explanation but calls the claimed resemblance something Scripture does not establish. His guesses about praying/not praying for James, exact midnight timing, Peter's inner fearlessness and Mary's relation to Barnabas are not public facts.

Actual Calvin at https://biblehub.com/commentaries/calvin/acts/12.htm : verse 5 first 1,700 characters; verse 15 first 3,700; verse 23 first 1,900. Verse 15 explicitly does not regard the group's reaction as proof of faithless prayer and rejects the deduction of one particular angel for every individual while affirming angelic protection. His guesses about their exact petitions and his conjecture that the second James is Alphaeus's son are not presented as Luke's statements. His verse 23 medical speculations and interpretation of Josephus's owl are not used.

Catholic primary paragraph 336 read directly at https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_one/section_two/chapter_one/article_1/paragraph_5_heaven_and_earth.html . It describes angelic protection beside each believer. The comparison clearly distinguishes official Catechism teaching from Calvin's individual commentary, rather than inventing a binding Reformed confession about guardian angels. No tradition is credited with teaching that all guardian angels look exactly like their charges.

Actual USCCB chapter 12 notes read at https://bible.usccb.org/bible/acts/12 . Their suggestions about public popularity or motive are not treated as independently established history. The note about 12:25 has different manuscript readings; the actual BSB wording is preserved, not replaced with the baseline's preferred quotation.

Independent ancient source: actual Josephus Antiquities 19.8.2 read at https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Josephus/ant-19.html . The first occurrence of silver was an unrelated passage about Caligula and was not used; the correct chapter 8 was subsequently located and read. It describes Agrippa at Caesarea, silver clothing, accepted divine acclaim, an owl, severe abdominal pain and death after five days. No worms in that account: the baseline's purported quotation with rotting flesh producing worms is not supported by the checked passage. Public prose distinguishes Josephus's details from Luke's. No diagnosis or claim that Josephus independently reports every supernatural detail.

## Acts 12:1–5
Plan section: included, because (b)/(c): Mark 10:35–40 supplies a supported connection between James and the cost Jesus said the brothers would share.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The baseline confuses Antipas with Agrippa in an introductory sentence; the public page keeps Luke's King Herod and identifies Agrippa only with checked Josephus. No precise Roman appointment history, ethnicity-based private insecurity or comprehensive genealogy is asserted. The BSB says after Passover, not execution on Passover itself. No invented plan to crucify Peter or guaranteed crowd demand copied from Jesus' trial. Exact guard shifts and unusual-prisoner classification are unverified; the four-by-four count is already in BSB. Luke does not say what prayers were made for James, his hopes, or the reason God did not rescue him. No statement that John died on Patmos or that James/John received specified right/left seats: Mark 10:40 does not assign them.

## Acts 12:6–19
Plan section: included, because (b)/(c): John 21:18–19 keeps this particular rescue within Peter's continuing costly calling.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No precise physical explanation of the guards' failure, angelic sleep mechanism, or gate engineering. Peter's sleeping does not prove fearlessness or spiritual negligence. Rhoda is the actual name, not the baseline's Rhonda. Her statement and the group's angel suggestion are reported rather than made a universal angel doctrine. Mary's wealth, first transition to house churches, exact visitor frequency, closest house, Peter's undisclosed destination and Luke's motive for withholding it are not established. No automatic identification of this angel as Christ from the separate burning-bush study. Peter mentions another James; no new owner position on the detailed identification is originated. Herod's execution order is not described as a documented Roman court martial or a known private conclusion of collusion.

## Acts 12:20–25
Plan section: included, because (b)/(c): Daniel 4 provides a labeled comparison of a proud ruler humbled before God, with the different outcomes stated.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Blastus is a royal official, not identified as a chambermaid. No verified payment or sexual favor is asserted from gaining his support. Exact food embargo mechanics, an unverified lexical claim that the address must be a lengthy scolding, or private sincerity of the acclaim are not public facts. Josephus's actual account does not contain the baseline's worms quotation; Luke's statement is kept distinct. No medical diagnosis or assertion that the ruler died instantly rather than after illness. The actual BSB 12:25 wording is retained through the manuscript/translation alternatives. No belief that future testimony will meet no persecution: James's death remains part of the same chapter.
