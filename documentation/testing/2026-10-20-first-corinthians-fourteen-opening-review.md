## 1 Corinthians 14:1–5

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): Acts 2:1–11 explicitly describes the Spirit’s gift making God’s mighty works heard across languages, placing communication within His witness among the nations.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The baseline’s reconstruction of every Corinthian counterfeiting tongues under peer pressure is not recorded as history. No claim Paul’s positive wish is necessarily sarcasm, an impossible Christmas wish or a refusal of every individual desire for gifts. Verse 5’s interpretation exception is retained; tongues are not declared inherently incapable of benefiting the church. Language-only and heavenly-speech interpretations are sourced fairly. Thayer’s account of ecstatic speech does not settle the entire debate, and a dictionary is not made a theological authority over Paul’s instructions. The baseline’s quoted Gordon Fee statement was not checked against Fee’s original publication and is not quoted or attributed as independently verified. No claim all practitioners of a modern church are deceptive or uneducated is introduced. Prophecy’s edifying function is explicit, but its modern operation is not settled as the site’s position.

## 1 Corinthians 14:6–12

Plan section: included, because (c): Nehemiah 8:7–12 supplies a direct earlier parallel of God’s word explained, understood and bearing fruit in the people’s joy and practical sharing.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The music examples concern distinct, meaningful sound; no detailed ancient orchestral system or exact military signaling history was independently verified. Paul’s speaking-into-air picture is not turned into an ancient scientific theory of sound waves or a claim sound never physically reaches listeners’ ears. Foreigner is explained from BSB main text/footnote without using barbarian as an endorsement of ethnic inferiority. Babel is not used to claim most current languages have persisted unchanged since Genesis 11. No exact linguistic test based on a few repeated syllables is supplied to authenticate or reject every modern claim. The baseline’s broad counterfeit reconstruction remains unverified and omitted. The earlier parallel is not an asserted prediction of Corinthians or proof the two events have identical circumstances.

## 1 Corinthians 14:13–19

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): Colossians 3:12–17 explicitly joins Christ’s word, teaching, song, thanksgiving and one-body life under love and peace.
Position source: Worship as the Early Church Knew It — content/studies/worship.md lines 435–444, especially its direct 14:19 interpretation.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Asking for interpretation is not proof interpretation cannot be a distinct gift; Paul lists the functions distinctly in chapter 12. The baseline’s statement that gifts cannot be sought through prayer is not imposed against verse 13. Wesley’s account of the speaker understanding but not benefiting others and the USCCB note’s account of speech without ordinary understanding are distinguished. Neither is presented as a unanimous interpretation. An audible Amen is not by itself proof a ministry has taught truth, and its absence does not prove no benefit. Spirit and mind are not made enemies, and all emotion is not dismissed. The owner position is limited to intelligibility/shared benefit, not a new doctrine of private tongues, gift cessation or an exhaustive service format. No new policy on every prayer request is derived from the related prayer link.
