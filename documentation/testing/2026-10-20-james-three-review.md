## James 3:1–2

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Luke 12:47–48 links entrusted knowledge with greater responsibility before the Master.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Calvin explicitly differs from the usual teaching-office reading, favoring self-appointed harsh moral judges; represented as his reading rather than Reformed dogma. Wesley reads the teaching role. Baseline claims that following an approved curriculum exempts someone from stricter accountability are not established by these verses; omitted. Neither a subjective feeling nor a claimed gift certifies someone's interpretation. Generic claims about every ancient Greek/Jewish intellectual or every rabbi's motives need independent historical sources and are not used. The baseline's blanket historical account of Rome permitting no ordinary Scripture reading is not independently established and is not repeated. James includes himself in we; Wesley's rhetorical exclusion of apostles is not silently made the text's only meaning. No owner decision on teachers' eligibility is created.

## James 3:3–8

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Galatians 5:22–25 places gentleness and self-control in the Spirit's fruit, supplying a positive direction beyond exposure of human failure.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline horse-rider/ship-captain identification with the Spirit is a teaching illustration, not an explicit identification made by James. The public plan instead cites the Spirit's actual named fruit. James' animal list can be compared with creation, but need not prove an exact deliberate four-part taxonomy or domestication of every species. Baseline restless means absolutely unrestrainable is not a sufficient lexical claim; the actual entry allows restless/unstable. No Gehenna garbage-fire history is treated as independently verified. The actual warning is not reduced to certain loss of rewards with no salvation relevance for anyone; that is the baseline's framework, not a verdict established by this passage. Shipwreck of Hymenaeus/Alexander is not diagnosed solely as an ungoverned tongue against the full 1 Timothy context. No blanket rejection of practical counseling or speech habits as help is inferred. The passage calls for responsibility, not an excuse to keep harming people.

## James 3:9–12

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Genesis 1:26–28 gives creation background; Colossians 3:8–14 explicitly connects renewal in the Creator's image with putting away destructive speech and putting on love.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Calvin and Wesley discuss the image damaged/lost in some respects with dignity remaining. The public agreement is confined to the dignity James invokes, not a claim that all traditions agree on the full theology of original sin. Cursing concerns contempt/hostile speech and is not narrowed to a list of forbidden sounds. Correcting harmful conduct is not prohibited by an invented never-say-anything-negative rule. The natural images do not prove that Christians never speak inconsistently; the imperative explicitly addresses that inconsistency. No claim that one lapse annuls every saving purpose of God or that worship makes other conduct irrelevant.

## James 3:13–18

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Matthew 5:9 connects peacemaking with being called God's children; Galatians 5:22–23 connects the character James describes with the Spirit's fruit.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline reconstruction of specific church-office campaigns, exact debate subjects and no NT Scripture yet is not established; omitted. General claims about Jewish/Greek wisdom being nothing but long speeches are not verified history. No teacher's family appearance, audience size or immediate conversion count becomes an infallible test of every teaching claim. The text's traits apply to claimed wisdom, not only formal teachers. Accommodating is not gullibility or approval of wrongdoing; purity and righteousness remain in the passage. Actual Thayer 87 and both commentators do not support replacing BSB impartial with a claim that only unwavering is right. Humility and teachability are retained without endorsing every proposed correction. Evil conduct is not turned into a diagnosis of demonic possession. Peace is not used to suppress honest correction or reports of abuse.
