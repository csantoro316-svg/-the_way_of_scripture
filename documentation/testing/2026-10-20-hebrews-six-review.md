## Hebrews 6:1–3

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). 1 Corinthians 15:1–4 retains received gospel foundation; Ephesians 2:8–10 joins grace/faith and the prepared fruitful life.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baptisms may involve instruction about other washing rites; not proof of multiple Christian baptisms, an exact required sequence for every Christian, only gift reception, or only one modern initiation system. Teacher's explicit rapture-only identification of resurrection of the dead is too narrow and unconfirmed by this sentence. Eternal judgment does not itself provide the entire proposed timetable of hell/lake-of-fire or settle every tradition's account of final punishment. No owner verdict on conditional immortality or universalism created.

Leaving basics means building further, not rejecting repentance, faith or resurrection. No mandatory comprehensive mastery of later jargon, assumption all doctrinal disagreements show laziness, or unproved chronology that a specific other epistle was certainly already circulated here. If God permits expresses dependence, not proof of a fixed present spiritual-IQ rank transferred unchanged into eternity.

## Hebrews 6:4–8

Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). John 15 fruit/care/warning is a labeled related picture; Colossians 1:9–12 describes God's positive purpose for knowledge, worthy life, endurance and gratitude. Neither parallel is claimed to settle the entire apostasy debate.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline's certainly-saved/reward-only interpretation is **one reading**, not consensus. Its assertion no unbeliever is ever enlightened or experiences any Spirit-related benefit is not a lexical proof and conflicts with the distinction Calvin explicitly makes. John's enlightens everyone in 1:9 cannot simply be assumed to mean every recipient already regenerate. Heavenly gift need not be identified exclusively as faith by a necessary sequence; actual primary readings differ.

Its no-second-initial-reset interpretation differs from Calvin, Wesley, Chrysostom and even Wilkin's human-impossibility account; not silently published as the only explanation of impossible. Neither impossible=merely difficult nor every ordinary sin permanently excludes repentance is asserted as consensus. No owner salvation/perseverance or restoration conclusion originated. Baptismal, regenerate-apostasy and temporary-participation readings fairly separated.

BSB's curse is imminent not merely resembling an unbeliever; burning's severity retained. Neither all burning=hell nor all burning=believer's reward-only purification is a lexical rule. No claimed statistical most common biblical fire use without audit. No unverified automatic ancient farming requirement to burn a field for next year's planting, no implication the text promises a new harvest immediately after the burning. Conditional hope of divine restoration belongs to Wilkin's named reading; not a reported event in Hebrews.

No rule that only current believers can shame Christ, that anyone who struggles is almost certainly a poor Bible student, or that all hearers outside a particular verse-by-verse class are uniquely endangered. These are teacher deductions, not independent checks. The warning remains serious and directed to the hearing community without clinical diagnosis or a test of someone's salvation merely because they ask questions.

## Hebrews 6:9–12

Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). Galatians 5 Spirit/serving/fruit and Matthew 25 care recognized by Christ connect love with God's wider purpose.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: We is not proved a committee of apostles. Confidence for better things is not explained as exclusive prophetic certainty or a guarantee attending this lesson fixes every future problem. Salvation-associated things do not mean only the numerical maximum of optional rewards; public retains ongoing faith/hope/love. No blanket instruction to care only for church members or to avoid all ministry outside it; Calvin expressly extends love beyond the household. No shopping/coupon analogy or quantified hierarchy of volunteers. God's remembrance is actual text, not a mechanical reward calculator.

## Hebrews 6:13–20

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Genesis 22 oath/blessing for nations explicitly taken up here; Galatians 3 connects Abraham's promised blessing with Christ. Hebrews 9:24/10:19–22 expressly develop entrance into God's presence for His people.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No exact thirty-year waiting claim imported from Wesley or conflation of the Genesis-22 oath after Isaac's birth with the initial promise before his birth. Hebrews 11 itself distinguishes received pledges from still-awaited completion. No undocumented universal ancient oath rule that a false speaker's life was always legally forfeited. Human oath analogy is not an owner ruling on contemporary oath-taking traditions.

Anchor is a metaphor connected with heavenly sanctuary, not proof of a detailed navigation mechanism or autonomous power of human optimism. Hope includes Christ's promised salvation, not solely reward maximization. No invented percentage of Israelites excluded from inner sanctuary, assertion every old-covenant worshiper had no genuine spiritual comfort, or confusion of high priest's bull blood for himself with all details of the people's goat offering. These ritual details must be checked in Leviticus rather than copied from the baseline.
