## Romans 8:18–25
Plan section: included, because (c): Genesis3's curse and Revelation21's renewal give the wider creation/decay/hope connection.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No specific millennial resurrection timetable, guaranteed immediate relief, or judgment that grief indicates unbelief. Baseline every particular suffering is the only possible way God could achieve His aim is not verified by the text. Childbirth image does not authorize medical claims or reluctance to relieve suffering. Adoption's future completion does not establish an independent Roman legal-history claim.

## Romans 8:26–27
Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): Romans8:34 explicitly adds Christ's intercession to this chapter's Spirit help and family relationship.
Position source: Prayer and Faith / What Does Prayer Look Like?.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline Spirit requests specific trials or disease for particular believers is not stated here; omitted. No assertion all audible requests are forbidden or groans require a particular manifestation. The Spirit's will-aligned help does not promise every requested outcome.

## Romans 8:28–30
Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): Ephesians1 explicitly places adoption/redemption/inheritance within God's purpose in Christ and uniting all things in Him.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Foreknowledge/predestination vocabulary alone cannot verify a full Reformed, Wesleyan or Catholic system. No site position originated. Baseline attributes Acts4:27–28 prayer to Stephen; actual Acts4 context is the gathered believers, so that attribution is excluded. No claim God calls evil itself good or that every painful event has a known specific explanation. Catholic600/1037 used within their scope, not presented as resolving all Catholic theological schools.
