Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Calvin and Wesley explicitly include the church/gospel era and people within the religious community; the baseline's exclusive unbelieving outsiders interpretation is not consensus. No date prediction or necessary modern political identification is added. The baseline's assertions about a uniform measurable worsening of all history, universal Pax Romana expectations, modern house sizes, disease rates, school violence, addiction and other numerical/cultural comparisons were not independently verified and are left out.

The baseline's equation of self-esteem with every listed vice, claims that depression proves disappointed pride and sweeping social-media diagnoses are not established by the text or independently checked sources. They are not public psychological or medical guidance. The list's actual self-centeredness, greed and cruelty are explained in ordinary language without a clinical diagnosis. No word-by-word etymology, hidden 19-step causal sequence, blanket ban on every pleasure, claim that all resistance to every authority is wicked, or allegation about every member of another faith is invented. The call to turn away from deceptive godliness is retained alongside the previous chapter's gentle correction.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Exodus does not name the magicians Jannes and Jambres; Wesley, Calvin and USCCB connect the names with them. No assertion that Paul must have used a particular surviving Jasher document or that all later details have canonical authority is made from the BSB footnote. The names' complete historical transmission has not been independently reconstructed. No exact number of Pharaoh's magicians is deduced from Exodus, no sleight-of-hand mechanism is proved, and Aaron's actual staff is not mislabeled as Moses' staff in the checked episode.

The baseline's household workday, all husbands-away-in-fields reconstruction, universal ancient female educational restrictions and claim that these women specifically became the teachers of 1 Timothy are not established. The passage describes particular vulnerable women; it does not make all women incapable of faithful understanding. Lois and Eunice's actual positive example remains visible. No known list of the women's exact desires or modern-equivalent diagnoses is invented. The baseline's claim these opponents can never be reached or must be permanently denied repentance is not made an owner position. The warning and eventual exposure remain, without promising immediate exposure in every case.
