## Exodus21:1–11

Plan section: included, because (c). Gal4 adoption and1Cor7 freedom/belonging-to-Christ develop the wider biblical theme without claiming Paul directly interprets this law.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline all slavery voluntary or equivalent to secure employment conflicts with actualEx21:4,7;22:3;Lev25:44–46. Excluded. Family separation, sale and property status described directly. Release is in seventh year after six, not after seven completed years. No invented wife-release price mechanism, universal bride purchase, child-marriage age, inability of all ancient women to work/own property, or superiority to every other ancient code. Historical assertions not independently established were left out.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: BSB for-life wording preserved. Rashi Jubilee and Wesley death-or-Jubilee are documented readings; lexical duration alone does not settle that legal question. No semantic code dividing Greek slave/bondslave into two religious classes. No claim God created slavery solely to explain Christ, or that a believer's belonging to Christ vindicates every human slave system.

Altar protection not restricted to religious officials. No unsupported claim capital punishment always deters or contemporary criminal-law advice.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: BSB21:21 says servant gets up after a day or two; not replaced by another translation's later death. Ownership rationale retained without portraying it as ordinary employment or claiming it proves masters never abused people. Release for destroyed eye/tooth retained. No age-of-majority doctrine derived from child vocabulary.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Pregnancy readings checked in actual Calvin/Gill/Rashi. Wesley has no22–23 comment and is not used for that dispute. No new owner abortion/personhood position supplied; no modern denomination made to speak unanimously. Mishnah compensation includes pain and humiliation, so baseline dismissal of these as merely modern inventions excluded. No inference that every death penalty could be replaced by money.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Thirty shekels/coins parallel does not establish equal purchasing power/currency across centuries or make the ox an allegory for Roman executioners. No invented priest-death atonement theory. No current Texas self-defense law, war policy or personal legal permission imported from ancient cases. No forced typology when direct NT connections suffice.
