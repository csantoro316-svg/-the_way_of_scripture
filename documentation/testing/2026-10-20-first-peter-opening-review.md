## 1 Peter 1:1–2

Plan section: included, because (a)/(b)/(c). Exodus 24:3–8 gives the covenant-sprinkling background; Hebrews 12:24 explicitly connects Christ, the new covenant and sprinkled blood.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline's exclusive claim foreknowledge must imply one precise election scheme is not consensus. Calvin/Wesley/Westminster/Catechism directly checked and fairly attributed. No human merit credited as the cause of grace in the Wesleyan/Catholic descriptions. Audience Jewish/Gentile remains a real interpretive question; neither all actual immigrants nor exclusively one ethnicity is asserted. Exact delivery route is not established by the list. Peter's death upside down in Rome around AD64 is not independently verified in the work here and unnecessary; omitted. Never denying/failing again conflicts with the correction in Galatians 2; omitted. Peter as first ever to confess Christ is not established against John 1. John 21 more than these means specifically fish is disputed; no private despair motive or return-to-work meaning asserted. General traditional authorship is not made a precise settled date. Literal blood carried to a material heavenly altar was already flagged in Hebrews and is not a textual detail in this greeting. Exile imagery is not a ban on neighborly service or lawful civic responsibilities.
