## Galatians 2:1–5

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c):Eph2:11–18 explicitly describes Jews and Gentiles brought near through Christ, joined in one body and given shared access to the Father through one Spirit. This adds the wider gathering purpose to Titus's case.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Identifying this meeting precisely with Acts15 or Acts11, the starting point of fourteen years, and an exact date are uncertain. Wesley1 favors Acts15; USCCB1 discusses chronological alternatives. The public account uses Paul's stated sequence without imposing one identification. A revelation is not automatically a visual experience with invented content. Titus being brought as a consciously designed test is a teacher's inference, not Paul's stated motive. Blanket historical claims that all Jews never ate with Gentiles or welcomed them home were not independently checked and are omitted. The fourteen-year interval does not prove Paul made no other Jerusalem/temple visit or settle all Torah observance questions. No full division of moral/ceremonial law is originated as the site's position.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Different audiences are not different saving gospels or exclusive ethnic territories. Acts10 and Paul's synagogue preaching already checked prevent that simplification. James's first position in the list does not by itself prove every detail of an office hierarchy. Wesley9's bishop terminology is his historical interpretation; the public shared reading concerns recognition and fellowship. The baseline's claim pillars literally means tallest poles is not lexically checked and is not used. Peter as the author of Mark is not established; the canonical author designation is Mark. Exact causes and extent of every Jerusalem believer's poverty and apostles' fear that Paul would neglect them were not independently established. God's working through both ministries does not support stereotypes of all Jews as stubborn or Gentiles as ignorant. The owner position is attributed and limited, not presented as the only recognized church teaching.

## Galatians 2:11–14

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c):Acts10:34–48/11:15–18 explicitly describes God's gift of the Spirit and repentance leading to life among Gentiles. That earlier recognition of God's welcome adds the larger divine purpose behind the practical fellowship at issue.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The text says men came from James; it does not establish that James authorized every demand or endorsed Peter's withdrawal. USCCB12 allows either actual representatives or claims of association. Communion as the precise meal context is possible, not stated. Peter's exact emotional response, a reported successful reconciliation and every onlooker's reaction are not narrated. Paul names hypocrisy and fear; this does not make all questions about food customs or all voluntary Jewish practice hypocrisy. Broad accusations against today's Messianic groups require their own primary documents and are omitted. This conduct dispute is not made an unresearched argument about every claim of papal authority or infallibility. No new owner position on public discipline is originated.

## Galatians 2:15–21

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c):Rom6:1–11 explicitly relates participation in Christ's death and resurrection to newness of life. Its resurrection setting develops the purpose of living to God beyond the immediate justification dispute.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Primary documents establish actual differences while all three accounts locate salvation in Christ and grace. Catholic justification is described as forgiveness and inward renewal, with grace-enabled faith/love and baptism; it is not caricatured as buying salvation without grace. Westminster's credited righteousness and faith alone are distinguished from inward renewal without claiming that all renewal begins only after justification;chapter13 presupposes regeneration and describes further sanctification. The baseline's lexical claim that the Greek simply means acquitted is not used as a one-word proof deciding the whole disagreement. Its universal claims about all Gentiles' moral condition and all Jews' views are not adopted. Different speech punctuation remains acknowledged privately; public exposition follows Paul's reasoning without assigning a certain endpoint to the quotation. No new site position on justification, baptism, Torah observance or the exact mechanism of union with Christ is originated.
