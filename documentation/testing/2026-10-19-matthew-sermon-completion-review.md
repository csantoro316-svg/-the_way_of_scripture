## Per-group decisions

### Matthew7:12 — Treating Others as We Wish to Be Treated

- Plan section: included, because(c). Philippians2:3–8 grounds concern for another's interests in Christ's servant life/cross; Galatians5:13–14 connects freedom with serving in love and Law fulfilled. Adds Christ's saving example and the people His freedom creates beyond repeating considerate conduct.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: AlexanderSeverus naming the GoldenRule/engraving gold palace walls has not been confirmed in an ancient primary source; neither it nor the bishops' note's competing naming chronology is published as established history. The repeated Law/Prophets language at5:17/7:12 is checked, while the technical term inclusio is unnecessary public vocabulary. No claim selfish preferences automatically determine morality, that imagined reciprocity excuses harmful acts, or that following a mental trick guarantees every correct answer. No precise reward calculation, unrecorded audience motive, or blanket literacy assessment.

### Matthew7:13–14 — The Way That Leads to Life

- Plan section: included, because(b)/(c). John10:7–10 gate/life and14:6–7 way to Father are supported parallels; Hebrews10:19–25 explains access through Jesus' blood and perseverance/love. Public wording identifies related pictures rather than claiming identical Greek terms or a hidden correspondence for every detail.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: exact city-gate counts, narrow refuse/shepherd paths, locations, and universal ancient traffic practices lack independent evidence here and are omitted. No universal Pharisaic assurance of salvation by ethnicity, Abraham rescuing all Jews from hell, or all crowds' identical belief is established. Minority status does not prove truth or popularity falsehood; no reverse popularity test. No fixed saved-population ratio, precise eternal-punishment mechanism, or claim this word alone decides a tradition's whole salvation doctrine. The text's serious life/destruction contrast retained.

### Matthew7:15–20 — Recognizing Teachers by Their Fruit

- Plan section: included, because(c). John15:1–8 joins fruitful life with remaining in Christ/Father's glory; Galatians5:22–25 names the Spirit's fruit. Adds the saving source and character of changed life beyond an external teacher-rating method. Related vine/tree images not collapsed into one detailed allegory.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: every false teacher necessarily unregenerate, every word ever spoken by one false, and a blanket rejection policy based on one error are not established by this image. Wesley explicitly allows true doctrine through flawed people; Matthew23:1–4 and1Thessalonians5:19–22 were checked before omitting simplistic identity-based rules. Neither all fruit means only converts' numbers nor only private moral behavior is stated as a universal tradition view; actual interpreter emphases named. No invented inner motives, classifying every Pharisee the same, numerical popularity proof, or private teacher blacklist. No claim John15 directly explains every Matthew image.

### Matthew7:21–23 — More Than Saying Lord

- Plan section: included, because(c). Titus2:11–14 connects Christ's gift of Himself with redemption from lawlessness and a people eager for good works; Ephesians2:8–10 connects gifted salvation with new life for good works. Adds redemption's purpose without selecting a new site account of faith/merit or assurance.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: speakers' miracle claims are reported; the text does not independently narrate every event, certify every claim, or identify all powers as demonic. The baseline's leaders-only exclusion of personal self-examination is not consensus; Wesley applies it personally. No equation of impressive ministry with acceptance, nor every failed action with final rejection. Psalm6's actual BSB verse8 checked; do not import different tradition verse numbering or claim the whole psalm describes David's final judgment. Never-knew wording does not by itself settle every tradition's account of perseverance, baptism, or grace. Reformed, Catholic, and Wesley descriptions are attributed accurately, without caricaturing Catholic works as buying initial grace or Reformed faith as indifferent to obedience. No new owner position originated.

### Matthew7:24–27 — Hearing and Building on Jesus’ Words

- Plan section: included, because(c). Matthew28:18–20 places learning/obeying Jesus within His risen mission to all nations and His presence; Ephesians2:8–10 places the changed life within new creation by grace. This adds resurrection/mission and God's life-making purpose beyond the immediate hearing/doing contrast.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: no independent weather, construction, or valley-floor history checked; actual rain/water/wind images suffice. No claim wise hearers avoid every hardship or that any life setback proves they are the foolish builder. Acting on His words not reduced to only abstract mental agreement, nor silently made a newly invented salvation-by-achievement rule. The Sermon's final warning informs the image, but no private end-times schedule or storm-by-storm identity assigned. No detailed claim attributed to Wesley's bare Luke cross-reference.

### Matthew7:28–29 — The Authority of Jesus’ Teaching

- Plan section: included, because(c). Deuteronomy18:15–19 and its explicit application to Jesus in Acts3:19–26 place receiving His words within the promised prophet and blessing for earth's families; Matthew28:18–20 connects His authority with His risen worldwide mission. Acts explains the promise, not an asserted direct quotation of Matthew7.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: baseline telephone-game reconstruction, every rabbi forbidden to inspect Scripture or contradict predecessors, every hearer immediately understanding the same things, exact20-minute duration, and repudiation of Israel's entire religious system are not independently established; none published. Scripture's comparison with their scribes retained without blanket condemnation of Judaism or invented chains of authority. Catechism581 is a primary theological explanation, not independent verification of every ancient teaching custom. The proposed ten-miracle/ten-plague map and medical claims from8A319 are outside these verses and not used. No detailed site authority doctrine originated merely from broader related studies.
