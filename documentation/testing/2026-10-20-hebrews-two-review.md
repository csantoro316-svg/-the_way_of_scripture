## Hebrews 2:1–4

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Deuteronomy 18's prophet/listening promise is explicitly applied to Jesus in Acts 3; Acts 1 and 1 Corinthians 12 develop Spirit-enabled witness and distribution of gifts.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline restricts the warning entirely to attendees who were never believers. Actual text says we/us and primary traditions differ about perseverance. No owner ruling created. Drift imagery does not lexically establish every invented river, boat, bank and rock detail. Calvin/Wesley use vessel imagery; no nautical reconstruction presented as text.

That universal statement is not repeated: Leviticus 6:1–7 includes deliberate deception/false swearing followed by restitution and an offering, and Numbers 15's defiant sin must not simply be equated with every conscious wrong. The comparison in Hebrews states just punishment, not an itemized universal penalty. No modern gifts-ceased/gifts-required verdict derived merely from the confirming signs; gifts remain according to God's will. Polemical claims about all Catholic miracles are not copied. No known author identity inferred conclusively from we heard.

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Colossians 2 joins forgiveness/cross/victory; 1 Corinthians 15 anticipates death's final defeat; Hebrews 4 directly develops the merciful high-priest help.
Position source: none.

Baseline's Ezekiel 28 Satan identification and annihilation claim are not settled by Hebrews 2 and need a separate passage/tradition review.
