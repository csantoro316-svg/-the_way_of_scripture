Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The baseline’s language that earthly concerns are ended and the body does not matter is not turned into a denial of care for people or the body. The following household and service instructions make present responsibilities real. No rapture timetable or claim about a believer’s average lifespan is added. No philosophical definition of hidden life is invented beyond belonging with Christ and appearing with Him.
