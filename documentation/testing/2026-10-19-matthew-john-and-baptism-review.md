## Per-group decisions

### Matthew 3:1–6 — John Calls Israel to Repent

- Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Isaiah40's comfort/preparation context and Matthew's explicit identification; Malachi4 plus Jesus' own Matthew11:10–14 and17:10–13 identification of John's Elijah role. Emphasis on preparing for Jesus, not simply unusual clothing or a self-contained religious movement.
- Position source: Baptism — sections I–II, immersion and believers' death/resurrection picture. Attributed narrowly; John/Christian baptism distinguished with Acts19.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Mary/Elizabeth as first cousins, John as cousin once removed, childhood play and later separation, Joseph's death timing, everyone except parents unaware of Jesus, exact months preaching and age when John moved to desert are not established by checked texts. Luke3:23, not John's Gospel, supplies age about30. No unsupported age13 bar-mitzvah or age17 trade apprenticeship. The isolated wilderness life and locusts stuck in beard are imagined; no claim John never contacted people or all Pharisees possessed formal top-school qualifications. Genesis descent/Christ power speculation from lesson introduction is not adopted. Ritual impurity is not simply moral sin; universal need for flowing water and all Jerusalem pools under hostile authorities were not independently verified; omitted. John3:25 mentions ceremonial washing but does not establish that reconstruction. Acts19 disciples' precise departure date, personal baptism by John and every missing teaching are not narrated. Complete novelty of John's washing, universal tax/tithe numbers, and a single dominant messianic expectation are not established. Repentance is not reduced to changed intellectual opinion; lexicon and Luke's concrete fruit matter. No blanket claim all Jews were godless or no true revelation existed for400years. Detailed kingdom timing/system not imposed on the page from one teacher. Fixed fourfold rabbinic interpretation scheme as the mandated method in this Gospel remains unverified and absent. Matthew's text provides its own Isaiah identification.

### Matthew 3:7–12 — Fruit That Shows Repentance

- Plan section: included, because (a)/(b)/(c). Acts1:4–5 explicitly repeats water/Spirit contrast, followed by Acts2; Malachi3:1–4 and4:1–2 support labeled coming/refining/judgment parallels. Common section explains actual fire differences; plan adds the wider promise movement rather than choosing the site's conclusion.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: the PDF's eye movement separating audience into saved people for Spirit then hostile leaders for fire is imagined, not Matthew's description. The two-group interpretation is not falsely attributed verbatim to Gill. Exact religious/political genealogy from Ezra, all Pharisees rich hypocrites, Sadducee control of all priests/guards/funds/courts and numerical Sanhedrin majority, universal20–30percent temple income, modern conservative/liberal party map and stated personal motives are not independently checked/used. No claim all Jews taught ancestry automatically saves every person or that one later rabbinic quotation proves universal first-century belief. A stones/children Hebrew sound-play may be proposed but Matthew's Greek does not establish John's exact original wording; no public word claim without lexicons/direct source. John names snakes' offspring, but the page does not turn that into an independently established literal spiritual parentage classification for every Pharisee or Jewish person. Baseline claims about Mormons in Utah, Muslim deity in Mecca or Catholic God in Vatican are unfair caricatures, not their own official doctrine, and are excluded. No assertion Jesus affirmed nobody, insulted all religious training, or made lack of education a qualification. Coming-to-baptism phrase does not prove every participant's secret motive or a personal divine warning to John; BSB wording retained. Sandal customs cited secondhand in Gill are not presented as checked Talmud history. The wheat/chaff explanation follows the text and actual implement entry without unverified crop techniques. Fire differences are fairly described, with no binding new site position or end-times date invented.

### Matthew 3:13–17 — Jesus Is Baptized

- Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). Psalm2 royal Son and Isaiah42 Spirit/delight servant are labeled connections; Matthew12:17–21 explicitly applies the servant text to Jesus. Philippians2:5–8 connects obedience with servant/cross without a loss-of-deity mechanism. Matthew28:18–20 expands Father/Son/Spirit and baptism into the commission.
- Position source: Baptism — sections I–II, mode/death-resurrection picture only. No new Jesus-specific doctrine originated.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: John1's next day is not proof the baptism occurred exactly the day before or that Cana occurred the following week; John3:22–24 explicitly shows John still baptizing after Jesus began work, so baptism did not instantly end/retire John. John's exact prior awareness, knowledge of sinlessness by childhood play, suspicion of nepotism and later question being about two universally expected Messiahs are not established. Matthew and John report different aspects; no total harmonized inner-knowledge timeline invented. Fitting uses prepō's contextual fit/appropriate sense, not another usage standing out as a literal instruction to display. Only John seeing/hearing the scene is contradicted by the BSB's Jesus/He wording and not proved by a singular pronoun; the page does not assign the entire crowd's awareness or physical hearing. Luke3:21–22 prayer/Spirit description does not establish Spirit as a two-way conduit without which the Son could not know the Father. Loss of divine form/power, a quantified first acquisition of miracle ability at baptism, all miracles inaccessible to Jesus independently, genetic/Adam-descent denial, or a complete incarnation mechanism is not established by Matthew/Philippians2 lexical gloss; WCF8.2/CCC536 and actual Scripture must remain visible checks. No supposed Spirit-decision explanation of Mark6's few miracles is invented. Genesis1's hovering is not a verified specific dove appearance or universal Jewish symbol by itself; speculative rabbinic identification and exactly first-of-three audible voices not used. Christian literally meaning little Christs/its universally proved original mocking intent is not established or needed. Water-to-Spirit spirit-immersion mechanics, automatic suspension of all further Christian service until baptism and every pouring/sprinkling act having no spiritual significance are one teacher's assertions, not conclusions of this page. Primary confessions explicitly differ on mode. Christian baptism and Christ's sinless obedience are not collapsed into an identical need for repentance. All quotations marked BSB use the checked wording, not the study's loose quotations.
