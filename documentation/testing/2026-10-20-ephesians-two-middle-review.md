## Ephesians 2:4–10

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): Titus3:3–8 explicitly connects mercy with Spirit-given new birth, renewal through Jesus, inheritance and a life devoted to good deeds, expanding the purpose of God’s gracious new life.
Position source: The Gospel According to Jesus33–55 / Baptism72–84; direct2:8–9 references, corroborated by I Am a Disciple28–30.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The baseline’s particular regeneration-before-faith scheme is not presented as every tradition’s meaning. It does not follow merely from applying every property of a literal corpse to spiritual death. PDF83 says Lazarus was dead three days; actual John11, previously checked in full, says four. No wrong chronology is copied. The statement that sitting always means finished work is not established as a universal biblical word rule. Spiritual seating with Christ is described as Paul does, without claiming believers have physically ascended or already possess every visible earthly office/property.

The whole salvation-by-grace-through-faith statement is God’s gift in actual Wesley8; Wesley10 also calls faith a gift. No isolated neuter-pronoun argument proves a complete election system. Baseline’s denial that anyone can speak of being saved by faith must be read alongside Scripture’s actual through-faith wording and previously checked Rom5:1/Luke7:50; the public page does not erase faith as the way the gift is received. Works are not broadened into a claim that the act of believing is necessarily an attempt to earn salvation. All three compared traditions describe grace through Christ; no blanket Catholicism=independent-self-salvation accusation is copied.

God’s workmanship is not lexically required to mean an exquisite masterpiece, nor does its relation to an English word prove a poem interpretation. Prepared good works does not create an unverified buffet/menu revelation system or a claim that churches sin by paying janitors or groundskeepers. No rejection of every ordinary opportunity for good because it lacks a private impression is invented. The actual gift and purpose of changed conduct remain together. Baseline’s precise allotment of rewards and ministry opportunities is not made a new owner position.
