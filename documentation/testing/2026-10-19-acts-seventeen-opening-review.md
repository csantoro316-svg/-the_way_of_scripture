## Acts 17:1–9
Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): 1 Thessalonians 2:1–2 explicitly recalls the earlier Philippi suffering and visit; 1:5–10 describes the wider witness that follows.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No exact road width, surfacing, length, complete route, population or bond conditions were independently verified; none is public. The absence of a mentioned synagogue in the towns passed through does not prove there was none or that no witness happened there. No professional paid heckler history or English rabble-rouser etymology is asserted. Leading women are not reduced to officials' wives. Luke records both Jewish and Greek belief; blanket ethnic motives are not assigned. The political charge is reported as the accusers' charge, not accepted as proven sedition. Luke's silence about miracles does not prove none ever occurred in the whole visit, and 1 Thessalonians 1:5 speaks of power and the Spirit without specifying every event.

## Acts 17:10–15

Position source: The Berean Standard.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Noble-minded is checked against the actual lexicon, not inferred as superior ancestry. No certainty the Bereans had mastered all prophecy, instantly understood every detail or never needed correction. The passage's commendation is not used to assert every church body denies examination or to originate a new owner ruling beyond the existing study.
