## Mark 1:35–39

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). John5 and Hebrews5 directly relate Jesus' earthly life/prayer with the Father and saving obedience. Romans10 explicitly relates sending/preaching/hearing/faith and adds the wider purpose of taking the message outward.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Thayer2614 explicitly treats Mark1:36 in a good sense. It does not prove hostility, annoyance, a rebuke of Peter's greedy fame-seeking or his later embarrassment. No mind-reading of companions' emotions or imagined Jesus speech about later ruling inserted into verse38. A solitary place does not establish a measured long distance, exact travel route or how long they searched. No claim this prayer was solely preparation for the leper episode, or that Jesus must replenish depleted divine power. John's account preserves unity of Son/Father; no functional denial of the Son's divinity or recorded content invented for this particular prayer. No universal rule all ministry success is measured by visible crowds, healings, or exactly this daily schedule. Romans10 is used for preaching and faith, not a new denominational pulpit policy.

## Mark 1:40–45

Plan section: included, because (a)/(b)/(c). Matthew11 explicitly names cleansing lepers among the coming One's works. Hebrews9 explicitly relates outward cleansing to conscience/service through Christ. End-of-story cost/change of places is labeled a reflection parallel, with actual reconciling death grounded in Romans5.

Position source: Worship as the Early Church Knew It — Matthew8:2, narrow humble approach of the same man; not a new site conclusion on all healing/ritual details.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The PDF incorrectly says compassion is orgizo meaning anger. Actual Thayer4697 supplies compassion at Mark1:41;3710 supplies anger. BSB's alternate reading concerns different underlying wording, not a hidden angry meaning of compassion. No claim certainty what the anger reading targets or that the BSB translates it wrongly. Leprosy is not assigned a modern diagnosis, automatic contagion on one touch, five-pace law or personal-sin verdict. No unverified first-century nationwide priest records, fixed universal ban from every city, or certainty the man failed to shout a warning just because Mark doesn't report one. No independent source establishes a mandatory three-Messianic-miracle test. The first-ever healed Jewish leper claim is contradicted by Miriam's healing/restoration in Numbers12; not published. No claim no priest had ever used Leviticus14. Actual14:8 permits return to camp but not tent for seven days, so the PDF's whole week outside camp is not copied. No ritual acts as cure; healed before examination. No compulsory one-to-one offering taxonomy into technical expiation/propitiation categories or literal transfer of bodily skin disease to Jesus. No statement Jesus morally violated God's Law by touching then commanded Law observance inconsistently. No inference every unhealed person lacks faith: own Catholic and AG documents explicitly qualify that, and no stereotype of all charismatic/Pentecostal/Word-of-Faith groups asserted. No claim leper's proclamation means spreading Christ's name is always wrong, or disobedience never affects real outcomes; Mark explicitly gives crowded-town consequences. No imposed claim every hidden religious leader motive, official Sanhedrin investigation stage or exact timing was proved by this event. No new healing guarantee/site position from related prayer study links.
