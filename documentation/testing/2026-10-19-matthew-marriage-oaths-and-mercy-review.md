- Plan section: included, because(c). Matthew19 returns marriage to God's creation purpose;Ephesians5:25–33 explicitly connects one flesh and Christ/church,self-giving faithful care. This is a Bible-wide marriage/redemption connection adding purpose beyond cataloging divorce rules; Ephesians does not explicitly quote Matthew5, so no stronger direct-reference claim. No new divorce rule derived from a comparison.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW:unverified blanket assertions that all ancient women could not own land/businesses,work,make contracts,testify,return to their father's household,or survive except by remarriage are excluded. No independently checked legal/economic source supports that universal picture. Likewise no settlements/dowry or child support anywhere is unverified. Ruth/Naomi are widows, not evidence of divorce economics. Exodus22 does not unconditionally require every seducer to marry regardless of the father's refusal. All Pharisees holding an identical easy-divorce rule, burned-meal details and a complete first-century Mishnah system have not been checked against contemporary primary evidence; no public claim uses them. The exception's exact scope and its effect on remarriage are genuinely disputed; lexicon range does not resolve them. Catholic bishops' Matthew5 notes on unlawful/incestuous unions were read as publisher exegesis, not a binding universal Catholic definition of porneia, and are not needed for this public doctrinal comparison. No claim every divorce has identical guilt/consequences or that all abandoned spouses are culpable. No universal Orthodox permission or universal modern Wesleyan view inferred. No owner position originated on the disputed exception or remarriage.
