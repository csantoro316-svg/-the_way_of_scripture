## Judges 20:18–48

Plan section: included, because (c). Prayer, offerings and Aaronic ministry lead into the wider biblical account of access through the permanent priesthood of Jesus. Hebrews 7 explicitly names Judah, Christ's lasting life and offering; Hebrews 4 supplies mercy and help in need. Not an assertion that Judah leading this battle explicitly predicts Christ.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Elohim in verse 18 does not prove the people sought an unknown pagan god or had no relationship with the LORD; the same verse names the LORD answering. No lexical diagnosis imported.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: First two replies lack a victory promise; the third includes it. Do not erase the LORD's actual instructions in order to call the entire campaign purely unauthorized. Wesley/Gill's humbling explanation is attributed, not a narrator diagnosis of each of the dead.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No 40,000 testing code, no claim all dead were personally the nation's worst sinners, no universal formula every defeat proves arrogance or every fast guarantees success. The narrator does not state Phinehas was absent from the earlier inquiries or that every approach to God requires this exact three-step ritual.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: BSB Bethel retained even though Wesley/Gill discuss house of God/Shiloh. No asserted exact tabernacle relocation chronology. Phinehas is Aaron's grandson, not Joshua's.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Retain all reported numbers exactly. Wesley proposes possibilities to reconcile initial numbers and later losses; no invented certainty about unreported casualties or new total substituted. The summary followed by detailed retelling is not counted as a second separate 25,000 killed.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Wesley's fury or possible law/oath explanation and Gill's anger/oath reading of verse 48 are directly checked. Deuteronomy 13 concerns an apostate city and does not automatically prove every act of this campaign legally authorized. No new site verdict adjudicating all combatant or civilian deaths.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Six hundred escape is stated; no conditional claim God's promises can fail, no annihilation theology imposed on all Benjaminite descendants. No modern instruction toward violence, no owner study edited, no owner position originated.
