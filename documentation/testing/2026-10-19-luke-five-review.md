## Luke 5:1–11

Plan section: included, because (a)/(b)/(c). Isaiah6 is an explicitly labeled parallel of confessed unworthiness and calling, not a claimed prediction. Acts2 shows Peter gathering people through the gospel; John21 is a separate later catch and renewed calling, explicitly a parallel.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: PDF says most crowd only wanted healing, but Luke5:1 explicitly says they press to hear God’s word; public follows text. No invented Andrew in boat, morning weather, James/John’s scorn, Peter’s private doubts/calculated cost or guaranteed maximum lifetime financial windfall. G1350 diktyon investigated as a proposed net claim only from PDF; no verified lexicon/history for its exact net type here, so dragnet-vs-casting net reconstruction omitted. No proof that every prior fishing failure was a deliberately arranged lesson, nor repeated-failure promise for all readers. No copied missionary snake anecdote without primary evidence. Matthew/Mark calling chronology differs among Calvin, Wesley and USCCB notes: no harmonization asserted as proven and no claim this was first meeting. No single required emotional conversion experience imposed. G2221’s alive sense supported, but no semantic claim that the word itself means every part of dead-to-life salvation theology. No speculative 153 numerology or merger of Luke5 and John21.

## Luke 5:12–16

Plan section: included, because (a)/(b)/(c). Jesus explicitly lists lepers cleansed as evidence of His mission in Matthew11; Isaiah35 supplies a labeled larger restoration pattern. No claim Isaiah35 explicitly mentions leprosy.

Position source: Worship as the Early Church Knew It, Matthew8:2, limited to the same man’s humble approach. Healing and priestly instructions remain open.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: PDF modern disease statistics, antibiotic regimen, immunity/transmission/incubation assertions not independently clinically verified and irrelevant here; omitted. Do not equate all biblical skin conditions with a single modern diagnosis. Lev13:12–17 allows whole-white skin to be pronounced clean; not a full clinical remission mechanism. No verified primary history for mandatory stoning/beating or burning every leper’s corpse/property; omitted. No compulsory one-to-one disease→sin allegory, skin-whitening→forgiveness or antibiotic→Spirit metaphor. PDF says no Jew ever healed before Jesus; Numbers12 Miriam is a scriptural counterexample (reviewed in earlier Mark research), so never-repeat claim excluded. No primary period evidence located for formal three-messianic-miracles investigation protocol; omitted, as in Mark. Cleansing procedure belongs Lev14, not source’s repeated Lev13 shorthand. No private manipulative dare/crowd-clearing tactic attributed to man. No all illness identifies personal punishment, no automatic guarantee of present healing, and no assertion compassion for bodily suffering has no value of its own. No universal timetable of death/rapture introduced. Luke16 explicitly prayer; unlike4:42, attribution belongs to Luke itself.

## Luke 5:17–26

Plan section: included, because (a)/(b)/(c). Isaiah35 labeled restoration parallel; Luke24 explicitly suffering/resurrection and forgiveness to nations.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No separately verified architecture for a central courtyard, trap door, stairs or exact roof composition, so public retains Luke’s tiles/lowering only. No invented Sadducees/Sanhedrin delegation or motive of formally verifying mandatory messianic miracles. Mark places parallel in Capernaum; no unnecessary exact building identification. Forgiveness before healing is explicit; private disappointment of paralytic or leaders being consciously baited/trapped is not. No proof particular sin caused this paralysis; no rule another person’s faith automatically grants salvation. No claim forgiveness is easy to accomplish: Jesus asks what is easier to say, and explains healing’s evidentiary purpose. No new sacramental absolution verdict drawn beyond scene.

## Luke 5:27–32

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Luke15 revisits same complaint and explains joy over repentance; Luke24 expands repentance/forgiveness to nations.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No independent history for particular Hazor–Damascus toll site, wheel/animal/cart tax schedule or Roman army directly enforcing Levi’s demands in Herod’s Galilee; omitted. General tax collector contempt shown by narrative, not proof every collector commits same extortion. No unreported Levi self-hatred, emptiness, revenge-tax spiral or motives. Matthew parallel names Matthew; no implied Bible account explicitly narrates Jesus renaming Levi. No blanket claims all Pharisees are knowingly fraudulent unbelievers or everyone in Jewish culture believed works earned redemption. No invented Pharisaic proposed back-row probation/restitution requirement. Physician image and repentance retain mercy with changed life, without replacing existing site position process.

## Luke 5:33–39

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Bridegroom removal points to narrated suffering; actual Luke22 states new covenant in blood, Hebrews8 God’s inward renewal/forgiveness. No own law/covenant system imposed by assigning every parable object a fixed counterpart.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No verified basis for every faithful Jew fasting twice weekly or wedding exemption from all rituals/restrictions. No unsupported precise wineskin technology or claim glass bottles did not exist; Luke’s stated effect is sufficient. Original text calls wedding guests, not brides. Fasting not reduced to mechanical training that inevitably makes Spirit take ownership of will, and no fasting regimen/medical advice offered. No claim Christianity must have no moral obligations or no relation to Ten Commandments; narrower Hebrews statement includes laws on hearts. No caricature of Mosaic Law as inherently evil, all Jewish people as identical, or every law-affirming Christian as unbeliever. Public notes fairly distinguish Calvin’s disciples-readiness reading from bishops’ newness reading; explanatory commentary ≠ universal official confessional decree. No final owner verdict on exact parable mapping.
