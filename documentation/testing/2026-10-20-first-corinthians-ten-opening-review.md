## 1 Corinthians 10:1–5

Plan section: included, because (a): Paul explicitly identifies Christ with the spiritual rock; John 6:31–35 connects manna with Jesus as the bread giving lasting life.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: A moving physical rock or a stream following every stage of Israel’s journey is not stated in Exodus. The USCCB mention of a rabbinic moving-well tradition was not independently checked against its primary text and is omitted. No claim that every Israelite was unbelieving or finally damned is imported; Exodus 14:31 explicitly says the people believed. The cloud shielding daytime heat, exact mechanics of baptism into Moses, and supposed water sprinkling were not independently established and are omitted. Shared sacramental comparisons are distinguished from agreement over sacramental effects. The baseline’s introductory Greek phrasing is not used as verified lexical analysis. A search lead for Catechism 1094 was not successfully read; no claim is attributed to it.

## 1 Corinthians 10:6–13

Plan section: included, because (a): Hebrews 3:7–19 explicitly applies the wilderness warning to believers sharing in Christ and calls for mutual encouragement.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Paul gives 23,000 deaths in one day; Numbers 25:9 gives 24,000 plague deaths. Wesley proposes adding those killed by judges; Calvin proposes approximate totals. Neither explanation was independently established as the historical solution, so no certain harmonization is offered. The passages’ actual figures remain unaltered. No blanket claim that pagan meals always included temple prostitution, or that Exodus 32’s revelry lexically means sexual acts, is verified or used. Numbers 25 does not describe the Moabite women as Israelite wives. The exact episode behind every complaint in verse 10 is not conclusively identified; Numbers 14 and 16 were checked and no single identification is insisted upon. Actual BSB verse 9 reads Christ; the Lord variant noted in its footnote is not substituted. Temptation’s lexical range does not establish a promise that every hardship stays within unaided human strength; 2 Corinthians 1:8–10 provides an explicit counterexample. No automatic chain from every pleasure to serious sin, fixed theory of each person’s eternal status, or unverified Lot parallel is imposed.
