## Acts 16:1–5
Plan section: included, because (b)/(c): 1 Corinthians 9:19–23 and Galatians 2:1–5 distinguish mission accommodation from imposing circumcision as a salvation condition.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Timothy's father is not declared dead merely from a past tense. His precise age, conversion date, mother's conversion by Paul and emotional condition are not narrated here. No universal genealogical rule that all people of mixed parentage must choose one identity is created. Paul's action is not made a new condition of salvation or proof the council secretly reversed itself. No guarantee that sound teaching always produces a fixed numerical increase.

## Acts 16:6–10
Plan section: included, because (b)/(c): Acts 1:8's outward witness continues through a particular directed journey and a call to preach.
Position source: Should We Seek Signs From God?
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The baseline identifies Spirit prohibitions with feelings, closed doors and inward peace, but Luke gives no such mechanism; no universal guidance formula is taught. No claim God has an error-prone process or that every inconvenience is divine prohibition. The nighttime vision is not automatically a dream or an angel in local costume. The narrator's we is visible, but a conversion of Luke at Troas or his exact prior biography is not inferred. No claim Peter hid or wrote both letters in Bithynia, or that Asia was permanently forbidden to the gospel.
