FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The baseline's absolute statement that Paul always prays the same knowledge prayer and never prays for physical relief/protection is contradicted by Romans 15:30–32 and even Philemon 1:22, where he hopes for restoration through their prayers. This does not cancel the actual positive prayer here. No new owner blanket prayer prohibition or absolute ban on requests is originated; the related prayer study is linked only for further reading.
