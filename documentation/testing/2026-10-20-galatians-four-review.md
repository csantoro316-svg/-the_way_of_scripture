## Galatians 4:1–7

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): Mark 14:36 records Jesus addressing His Father as Abba; Romans 8:15–17 joins believers’ address, adoption, shared inheritance and future glory. These connections extend the immediate account into shared prayer and hope through the Son.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The baseline says believers were always God’s adopted children even before faith and uses this to explain regeneration/election. Ephesians 1’s prior purpose does not by itself establish that exact chronology of actual adoption; Galatians 4:5 says receiving adoption. No new owner position or supposedly universal election model is supplied. The baseline’s Abba=Daddy wording is not a baby-only lexical meaning in the checked Thayer entry. Exact ancient inheritance ages and a legal timetable for every society are not independently researched and are not asserted. “Time fully come” does not supply a complete dispensation scheme by itself. Sonship includes women as explicitly stated in 3:26–29; no blanket claim about all ancient women’s inheritance rights is needed.

## Galatians 4:8–11

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): Colossians 2:16–23 connects shadows and Christ, regulations and the body’s growth from its head. This adds the larger Christ-centered source of growth beyond repeating the calendar warning.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The exact referent of stoicheia remains disputed; the actual BSB main text and footnote alternatives are distinguished. Wesley9’s elementary-law emphasis and USCCB3’s heavenly-powers preference are attributed rather than presented as one settled lexical fact or universal denominational definition. The baseline assigns every calendar unit to exact festivals/jubilees; those identifications are not established by the verse, and Wesley10 disputes sabbatical-year observance outside Canaan. No claim that every voluntary calendar observance is sinful is originated; Romans14’s freedom of conscience was previously checked. No broad modern group condemnation or automatic equation of all religious structure with false worship is imported.

## Galatians 4:12–20

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): Romans8:29 explicitly describes God’s purpose of conforming His people to His Son, adding the larger family purpose behind Christ being formed in the Galatians.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Illness is explicit; an eye diagnosis, an infirmary stay and identification with the thorn in 2Cor12 are not. The eye-giving statement can express strong affection and is not proof of a diagnosis. USCCB13 acknowledges differing proposals. No exact date, town, first/second-visit reconstruction or recovered medical history is supplied. The warning about these particular teachers is not expanded into an unverified claim that every religious leader wants power. Birth imagery is Paul’s comparison, not proof that the Galatians need a second conversion or that all spiritual formation has one fixed timetable.

## Galatians 4:21–31

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): Paul explicitly quotes Isaiah54:1, read in its1–10 restoration setting; Hebrews12:18–24 explicitly sets Sinai beside heavenly Jerusalem and Jesus the new-covenant mediator. These add the wider gathering/restoration hope.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Paul explicitly supplies the women/covenants, Sinai/present Jerusalem and free Jerusalem-above connections. The baseline additionally pairs Sinai with Calvary and identifies every character with a spiritual role. Calvary is not named in this passage; that additional teacher-created pairing is not attributed to Paul. The baseline identifies Sarah’s covenant precisely as Abrahamic; Paul’s promise setting is clear, but no complete disputed covenant-system identification is imposed as consensus. Genesis does not describe Ishmael as wholly devoid of blessing: prior Genesis17/21 include promises about him; no blanket “nothing lasting” or denial of God’s care for Hagar is copied. Paul’s contrast does not authorize ethnic contempt, mistreatment of Jewish people or a universal every-detail hidden-code method. No exact Roman political reconstruction, mountain-location argument or complete Israel/church end-times scheme is added. The public plan labels only connections actually supported in Scripture.
