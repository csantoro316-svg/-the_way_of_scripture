## Per-group decisions

### Matthew 4:1–11 — Jesus Resists the Tempter

Context follows actual three exchanges and their Deuteronomy settings; word note distinguishes test and enticing to evil; Matthew/Luke order difference reported without declaring a certain chronology or transportation mechanism. Shared common reading sourced to Catholic Catechism and Wesley, not presented as the baseline's disputed power model.

- Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). Forty days/Israel's forty years, three Deuteronomy answers and son/testing setting support the wilderness parallel. Romans5/1Corinthians15 explicitly establish Adam/Christ obedience and new life, applied as larger contrast rather than falsely said to recount these three events. Hebrews4:14–16 explicitly relates real testing and sinlessness to the help believers receive.
- Position source: Worship as the Early Church Knew It — final temptation4:9–10 only.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: the PDF's comprehensive Satan prehistory is not established by the checked texts. Ezekiel28 addresses ruler/king of Tyre and Isaiah14 king of Babylon; neither narrates an attempted physical seizure of a heavenly mercy seat. A possible further spiritual reading does not establish that throne coup, highest angelic rank, unmatched power/beauty, exclusive covering post, original name Lucifer, precreation sanctuary design, or all historical attacks' exact demonic instructions. No asserted Satan riverside location/ignorance of Jesus until baptism or secret listening transcript. Genesis6 does not establish a narrated order by Satan to mate with women; Cain/Pharaoh/Herod motives not all proved by an invented continuous biography. Christ as merely less powerful than Satan without divine powers, possessing exactly the same resources/mental limitations as every believer, hypothetical salvation disqualification mechanics and a quantified power hierarchy are not established here; real testing and sinlessness can be affirmed without those assertions. No claim Jesus was abandoned by Father/Spirit after the text says Spirit led Him. Luke4:2 depicts testing during the forty days, not only after them; exactly three lifetime opportunities is not claimed. Exact fasting physiology, forty-day universal exhaustion of body-fat reserves, cognitive impairment, five-week return of hunger, no medical danger, everyone normally working, and water consumption are unverified and not used/advised. Matthew does not narrate a direct mandatory fasting command whose time Jesus would shorten by eating; fasting is already described before the offers. Universal numerical40 code, one-to-one three-lust mapping, every modern hobby/disease as a demonic attack, and comparative ease of crucifixion versus obedience are not established. The garden was not described as infinite options and exactly one divine instruction in all senses. Matthew/Luke different order does not prove Luke alone has exact chronology. Temple southeast corner,170-foot height, mountain next to Jericho, exact transportation/vision means and Satan's changing knowledge remain unverified. Psalm91 has no narrated guarantee about every ox-cart accident or detailed conditional timeline protecting Jesus; its actual promise and Jesus' Deuteronomy response suffice. No assessment of every other religion's salvation teaching from caricature, no medical fasting advice, no new Christology or site position originated.

### Matthew 4:9–10 — Worship and Serve God Alone

- Plan section: included, because (c). Matthew28:18–20 and Philippians2:8–11 trace received authority/exaltation after obedience, contrasting the proposed allegiance without certifying Satan's legal property claim. This adds the Gospel's later direction beyond simply restating exclusive worship.
- Position source: Worship as the Early Church Knew It — Matthew4:9–10.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: all relevant final-temptation limitations above. Satan's statement is reported as an offer, not a verified absolute legal deed to every country or a reliable promise of everlasting rule. Every inheritance requiring the recipient's own death is not a valid universal legal principle. No assertion Satan ceases to exist, all wealthy/ruling people worship him, or the text explains his final destiny here. No exact territorial shares for each believer supplied by these two verses. The study's paraphrase is replaced with actual checked BSB, not mislabeled as translation. No new system of worship originated.

- Position source: Worship as the Early Church Knew It — Matthew4:10/allegiance-service paragraphs.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: final-temptation limits above apply. Previous LSB/inexact quotation replaced with actual BSB; geographical-gathering/John4 conclusions in the old source are not smuggled into this context as though spoken by Jesus here. ### Matthew 4:12–17 — Light Dawns in Galilee

- Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Matthew explicitly applies Isaiah9's light promise; its royal child/Davidic peace setting and Matthew28 mission develop the larger direction without imposing a postponed-kingdom scheme. Psalm/prophetic promises are not reduced to road maps.
- Position source: The Gospel According to Jesus — Receiving the Gospel; repentance/faith response only.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: precise post-temptation calendar/four Passover reconstruction, unmentioned travel and relatives' every move, John imprisonment date, Satan secretly causing every event, and lack of risk until the cross are not supplied by Matthew. Capernaum universally believing versus Nazareth never believing is too sweeping; Matthew11:20–24 explicitly warns Capernaum for not repenting. Via Maris as a specific named Roman route Egypt-to-Babylon passing exactly through the house, trader numbers, detailed strategic calculation, Sea etymology from harp, precise provincial boundaries and modern national water supply not independently checked/used. The chapter reports John imprisoned, not all Herod marriage details needed here. Baseline claim a national vote of repentance would have brought the literal millennium immediately instead of the cross is one teacher's reading, not Matthew's explicit sentence or shared church teaching. No alternate history declaring the cross a fallback plan, no asserted precise change in God's salvation purpose. Scofield primary-page retrieval attempts failed, so no recalled quotation/denominational attribution included. The public kingdom claim is restricted to the checked sources' broad present work and future completion; detailed disagreements remain for a sourced study.

### Matthew 4:18–22 — The First Fishermen Follow Jesus

- Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Matthew10 names these men and sends them into kingdom mission; Matthew28 expands the commission. John15:16 explicitly speaks of Jesus choosing/appointing for lasting fruit. Practical lesson: His call and training reach people in ordinary work, without inventing every disciple's private life.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: John1 names Andrew and an unnamed companion, not certainly Gospel author John; exact identification/timetable cannot be assumed. Matthew does not narrate every previous introduction, education level, immoral fisherman's lifestyle, opinions about full-time study, bills, diploma status or a universal formal rabbinic academy/career qualification system. Luke5's catch may be compared but cannot be asserted the unrecorded exact instant in Matthew without showing the argument; no private weariness with fishing attributed. John1's earlier meeting is actual text; no claim Jesus hypnotized strangers or that Matthew is unrealistic. All Christians abandoning employment/family responsibilities is not this specific call's universal rule. PDF conflates James son of Zebedee with later Jerusalem leadership associated with another James; not repeated. Andrew's entire Black Sea route, martyrdom tradition and exact site, Peter first Gentile conversion, all apostolic educational backgrounds and vocational histories require separate source checks; not necessary here. Jesus calls rather than proving no training/qualifications ever matter. No church leadership/ordination position invented from this scene.

### Matthew 4:23–25 — Teaching, Healing, and Growing Crowds

- Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Matthew9:35–38 repeats teaching/proclamation/healing with compassion;10:1–8 shares mission. Matthew11:2–6 explicitly points to the works as an answer about Jesus' identity, while11:20–24 distinguishes seeing miracles from repentance. These develop purpose and response beyond crowd size.
- Position source: The Gospel According to Jesus — Core and Fullness; Savior/King proclamation and His character only.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: 2,800square miles/3million Galileans, exact thousands/tens-of-thousands, walking distances, all crowd motives and every later decline in numbers are not independently established. Synagogue is Greek assembly, not a Hebrew etymology; its precise institutional origin in exile is not made certain merely because Thayer says seems. No blanket conversion-only program inferred from synagogue locations. Matthew distinguishes seizure/paralysis/demonic conditions; no diagnostic rule that all mental/neurological illness is possession, no ancient/modern cure statistics or medical advice. Healing real bodies is not dismissed as an unfortunate delay of death or as God deriving no good from someone's health. The text/9:36 show compassion as well as testimony. No guarantee every faithful person now receives immediate total healing, or every unhealed illness means inadequate faith. Nor does the text prove every person in the entire region was cured. Disciples can be learners without all sharing identical understanding, but no universal postgraduate-school process or precise crowd/disciples/apostles headcount is asserted. No unsupported rule that faithful teaching always makes an unbeliever either convert instantly or flee permanently; no condemnation of particular modern service styles. The lesson's contested reading of later John6/soil parables is not settled in advance here. No new healing/salvation position originated.
