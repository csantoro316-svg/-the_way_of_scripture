Baseline's internal event reconstruction is not treated as the Gospel's wording.

BibleHub BSB main text read and parsed: all Matthew 28; Luke 24:36–49; John 20:11–18 and 24–29; 1 Corinthians 15:1–8, 12–26 and 35–44; Daniel 7:13–14; Genesis 12:1–3; Galatians 3:6–9; Romans 6:3–4. Actual Thayer 3100 and 1365 entries read, not generated Topical Lexicon: making a disciple/teaching; doubting/wavering. Greek meaning does not determine the exact object of each disciple's doubt or cancel the command to go.

Primary own-tradition sources checked directly: Catholic Catechism 638–648 and 1246–1255; Westminster Confession 8.4 and 28.1–7; 1689 Baptist Confession 29.1–4; Methodist Articles III and XVII on UMC's actual Articles of Religion page; Wesley Matthew 28 notes; Lutheran Augsburg Confession IX on Concordia Publishing House; USCCB's Matthew 28 notes; Calvin 28 introduction and 16–20. ## Matthew 28:1–10

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). 1 Corinthians 15 directly explains resurrection's necessity and Adam/Christ's connection and future bodily life; women are sent with the announcement.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The PDF calls the other Mary Jesus' mother, whereas Matthew connects her with 27:56/61, not an explicit identification as Jesus' mother. No new anti-Catholic conclusion about Mary from which Gospel uses her name. No invented 4am departure, daylight safety rule, disciples mocking/discouraging the women, knowledge of guards helping, or proof they expected an opening miracle rather than intended anointing. No assumed exact sequence of every visit or claim Mary Magdalene certainly traveled alone: John 20:2 uses plural we. No assertion angel witnessed the physical instant of resurrection or opened the tomb to permit Jesus to escape. Matthew does not supply internal physiological heart/lungs/wrapping-removal sequence. No unverified exact worldwide geography of Sheol, timed tour and transfer of all Old Testament saints, or mechanical Christ-body-only-healed-versus-unrelated-new-body-for-us account. Catholic 645–646 distinguishes glorified resurrection from return to mortal life; 1 Corinthians 15 supports transformation, not invented mechanics. John 20:8 says the other disciple saw and believed, contrary to baseline's categorical neither man believed after inspecting the tomb. John 20:17 says do not cling, not a universal prohibition on touching contradicted by Matthew's feet and John 20:27. Luke's Emmaus account names Cleopas; no claim both men unnamed. No claim every faithful ministry step guarantees a visible miracle.

## Matthew 28:11–15

Plan section: omitted, because none apply beyond context and common interpretation here. Opposition to the announcement is explained without repetitive filler.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline says the cover story was quickly buried, while Matthew explicitly says it remained in circulation as he wrote. No adoption of that unsupported success chronology. Guards report what happened, but no claim they watched the instant Jesus' body rose. No automatic death penalty or unit size independently verified. No categorical leader-private-thought narrative, no requirement the governor actually heard and was bribed, and no transferring these particular actors' conduct to Jewish people generally. Jesus and His first followers are Jewish. Matthew's story does not logically make every historical question impossible. No invented dated witness census from Paul's approximate five hundred statement.

## Matthew 28:16–20

Plan section: included, because (a)/(b)/(c). Genesis 12's nations promise is explicitly connected with the gospel by Galatians 3. Daniel 7 universal dominion is labeled scriptural parallel. Matthew 1 Immanuel and 28 enduring presence frame the Gospel.

Position source: I Am a Disciple / The True Church According to Jesus / Baptism, limited to the existing conclusions above.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The PDF equates Matthew 28:9 with the eleven meeting in a Jerusalem upper room, but Matthew 28:8–10 refers to the women departing and Jesus meeting them. Draft follows Matthew. No exact Tabor identification or assumption this is Paul's five-hundred appearance. Thayer 1365 supplies doubt/waver, not certainty that only mission competence was doubted; Calvin describes hesitation recognizing Jesus and Catholic 644 relates resurrection doubt. No compulsory eight-day delay caused solely by Thomas' refusal to travel, or assumption Jerusalem appearances violate Luke 24:49 instruction to wait. Christ's authority reassures but does not erase responsibility, make mission easy or guarantee earthly resources/safety; no new site claim that refusing evangelism can never affect another's hearing or that reward is the only reason to obey. No unsupported three-stage rule that baptism is exclusively a community-membership witness or only possible public response. No claim all national groups exclude Jews; worldwide mission includes them. No new church staffing policy based on participles.

## Matthew 28:19–20

Plan section: included, because (a)/(b)/(c). Same supported connections as parent.

Position source: I Am a Disciple / The True Church According to Jesus / Baptism, as above.

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Genesis 12/Galatians 3 connect nations with God's promise; Romans 6 directly relates baptism to Christ's death/resurrection and new life.

Position source: I Am a Disciple / The True Church According to Jesus / Baptism, as above.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Legacy page mistakenly contains Acts 8:37 textual note and eunuch response under Matthew 28:19. Neither belongs to this verse; removed from this passage while preserving URL. No new textual variant claim made for Matthew 28:19. Mode/recipient/effect comparisons come from checked primary documents, not Greek word alone. No declaring an unworked infant-baptism site conclusion.
