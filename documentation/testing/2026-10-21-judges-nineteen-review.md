## Judges 19:1–30

Plan section: included, because (c). Ephesians 5 explicitly grounds protective marital love in Christ's self-giving for the church; Luke 10 and Hebrews 13 show concrete care and identification with the harmed. This adds the positive saving purpose beyond the account's failures. The contrast does not turn the woman's involuntary suffering into a divinely required saving sacrifice.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The Levite having another wife and committing adultery merely by this relationship is not narrated. His status as Levite does not prove Aaronic priesthood or establish every claim about his residence. BDB provides no warrant for inventing a separate first wife.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Phinehas in 20:28 is Eleazar's son and Aaron's grandson, not Joshua's grandson as baseline says. No exact date, longest-tribe population claim, or certain Samuel authorship retained.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The opening BSB unfaithfulness and Gill's discussed alternative are distinguished. No privately required execution, no claim reconciliation violates God's law, and no claim accepting an adult daughter at home caused her later rape. FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Travelers' supplies in 19:19 are theirs, not a survey proving the town has abundant food. No independent historical proof of no inns, specific travel times/distances, universal legal penalties for failed hospitality or every convention of honor. None retained.
