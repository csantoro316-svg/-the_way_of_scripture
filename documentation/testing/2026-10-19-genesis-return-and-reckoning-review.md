## Genesis 32:1–21

Plan section: included, because (c). Promise-based dependence and acknowledged weakness connect with Hebrews 4:14–16's mercy/help through Jesus. This is not called an explicit NT explanation of the livestock gifts.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: PDF's claim God takes an uncontrollable risk in revealing His plan is not established by Genesis. Exact motives for Esau's four hundred men, assumptions that every preparation proves unbelief, a legally binding reversal of birthright through respectful speech, and gifts exactly representing 2–3 years of wages are not given. Wesley instead regards peace preparations as prudent; public distinguishes fear/prayer/practical action without pronouncing every step a failure. No rule that appeasing a wronged brother is necessarily friendship with an evil world or repentance directed at the wrong person. The precise total valuation, army tactics, exhaustive reason God allowed the report, and an unreported first-ever prayer in Jacob's life were not independently proved, omitted. Actual livestock numbers preserved in BSB, rather than substituting the teacher's approximate count. God's camp is not proof angels are merely backup to Jacob's autonomous army.

## Genesis 32:22–32

Plan section: included, because (c). Hosea 12:3–6 directly revisits Jacob and calls Israel to return, love/justice, and waiting. 2 Corinthians 12:7–10 adds the wider sufficient-grace-in-weakness theme, clearly a comparison rather than Paul's exposition of the wrestling.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No exact reason Jacob was alone, intent to use the river as a shield while exposing his family, physical ambush mechanics, or objective brawl-for-survival description is stated. Touch/strike lexical range does not establish an exact medically necessary force, pain level, anatomical reconstruction, or permanent immobilization. BSB struck retained. No claim Jacob could definitely have ended it sooner by a particular prayer or that his full inward transition occurred at a precisely known second. Historical identity readings are presented fairly; none made a new site position. BDB's alternative Israel derivations do not prove the PDF's fixed God-fights-for-Israel interpretation or every later national/eternal inference. No invariant Jacob=sinful/Israel=godly name code: Genesis 35:22 itself uses Israel amid serious wrongdoing, and names alone do not settle character. The teacher says the food restriction disappeared from Jewish practice; that modern claim was not independently verified and is omitted, with public prose simply describing Genesis' stated memorial restriction. No inference that disability signals God's disfavor or that a believer must overpower God to receive grace.

No exact 1–2 year stay in Succoth inferred merely from building.
