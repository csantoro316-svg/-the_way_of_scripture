No sweeping claim about every pagan's prayers is independently established. No exact emperor, letter date or political chronology is necessary to explain rulers in the text. The baseline's polemic about other traditions' mediators is not copied. This passage explicitly calls for intercession for others while naming Christ the one mediator. No unverified account of Catholic, Jewish, Buddhist, Taoist or any other community's prayer is assigned from another teacher's description.
