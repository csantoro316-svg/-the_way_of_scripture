## Genesis 6:1–7

- Plan section: included, because (a). Jesus explicitly uses Noah's days in Matthew 24; 2 Peter 3 explicitly uses the flood and God's patience in the future-judgment teaching. Neither requires a full Nephilim reconstruction.
- Position source: none. Source studies searched for Noah/flood/Genesis 6; no developed conclusion on these disputed opening verses. Exact approved wording.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: baseline certainty that sons of God never describes humans is not established by BDB 1121 or broader biblical son language; not asserted. A plan to genetically corrupt the Messiah's ancestry, biological demon reproduction mechanics, universal wishes of Jewish girls, and a particular identification of Nephilim from etymology are not established by Genesis/Jude or BDB; excluded. Its claim that all relevant Greek copies read angels is not manuscript-verified; Augustine itself acknowledges differing copies, so no universal textual claim. Its treatment of the Numbers spies' entire Nephilim report as deliberate falsehood and a mandatory revised attachment of afterward are not established by the checked sources; not asserted. Both countdown/lifespan readings are directly sourced without a site verdict. God's grief is stated as narrative; no claim God is forced by events, learns surprise information, or authored sin is introduced. The PDF's detailed Antichrist death/resurrection/soul account and calendar are not established by the checked Noah/NT texts; excluded. Claims about fish lacking relevant blood/life classification are not verified by an independent zoological source and are not used.

## Genesis 6:8–12

- Plan section: included, because (a). 2 Peter 2 explicitly names Noah and rescue; Hebrews 11 explicitly identifies his faith in the warning and preparation.
- Position source: none. No developed site treatment of this character/favor unit. Exact wording.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: lexemes and verse order do not establish every disputed step of a systematic salvation chronology; no such inference. Nine as judgment, ten as testimony, and eight as a required hidden code are not established by the checked Scripture; excluded. Blameless as genetic purity is not supported by BDB 8549's moral usage. All eight passengers' inward spiritual states are not individually established by calling Noah righteous or by 2 Peter 2:5; no blanket claim. No assertion that every other creature/person had no conceivable value to God beyond what the actual judgment passage states.

## Genesis 6:13–22

- Plan section: included, because (a)/(b). 1 Peter 3 explicitly links the ark-building days, preservation through water, baptism, and resurrection. Ark as a picture of provided rescue is explained without a secret meaning for every construction feature.
- Position source: Baptism. Existing source, `content/studies/baptism.md`, 1 Peter 3:21 section, explicitly says the ark was God's appointed vessel, not wood possessing saving power. Only that limited developed point is used, and Baptism is linked in position and deeper study. The study's discussion already points to 1 Peter 3; no authored study prose is altered or new view invented.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: pitch and atonement as automatically the same word/meaning is not supported by BDB 3724's distinct entries. Cubit-to-modern dimensions, stability/capsize guarantees, exact species counts/capacity, all-vegetarian predators, no boats before Noah, one continent, universal fossil formation, and tectonic causes are not established by Genesis or the checked lexical entries; no independent engineering/geological/zoological basis established, so excluded. Actual numbers remain in BSB units. Unknown modern gopher-wood identification left unknown. Exactly 120 construction years and a precise completion threshold for the final appointed believer are not stated in the checked Genesis/2 Peter texts; excluded.

## Genesis 7:1–24

- Plan section: included, because (a). Matthew 24 and 1 Peter 3 explicitly use the flood and preservation.
- Position source: none. Baptism addresses a later water/resurrection comparison, not a settled view on every event, extent, or chronology in this whole chapter. Exact wording; no broad position inferred from the narrow preceding group's authorized point.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: the baseline's no-prior-rain claim, precise pre-entry timeline extended into a seven-year church removal, exact Resurrection calendar match, single-door imagery as proof of every permanence-of-salvation detail, and exact moment Methuselah died are not established by the checked Bible passages; excluded. John 10's shepherd illustration does not identify Noah's door as its referent. The physical flood model (water canopy/storehouse, continental breakup, mountain uplift, ocean trench creation, Grand Canyon carving, flash-frozen mammoths, all fossil strata, rain/evaporation impossibility) is a model proposed by the teacher, not a stated conclusion of Genesis/2 Peter; no independent scientific evidence establishing it was provided in the checked materials, so none is printed. Neither Augustine's obsolete physics nor Ross's scientific claims are adopted; only their own stated extent readings are represented. BSB clean-animal wording is retained; no unverified total-animal count asserted.

## Genesis 8:1–5

- Plan section: included, because (c). God's remembering/acting for deliverance recurs in Exodus 2 and Luke 1, adding beyond water recession as a single narrative event.
- Position source: none. Exact wording; no developed site study on this unit.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: precise calendar conversions making the seventeenth day an exact Resurrection anniversary are not established by Genesis or the checked NT source; excluded. A named modern peak/ark site, Ararat-specific pillow-lava proof, exact pre/post-flood mountain heights, new origin of weather, creation of named lakes, and ocean-trench drainage are not established by the narrative or independent sources consulted; excluded. Wind/spirit word range is checked without forcing wind to mean Holy Spirit. Remembering is explained from BDB II.1.a and narrative action, not an assumed failure of divine memory.

## Genesis 8:6–12

- Plan section: omitted, because none apply. The actual text describes Noah assessing the retreating water; no explicit NT explanation or sufficiently established raven/dove timetable was found. Generic patience would repeat the interpretation rather than add a substantive larger-plan connection.
- Position source: none. Exact wording.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: raven as Elijah, each dove as a phase of Spirit removal/restoration, olive leaf as 144,000 Jewish converts, and a coded Gentile-conversion schedule are proposed connections in the baseline; Matthew 3's dove and other isolated bird/tree passages do not establish those details in Genesis. Excluded. The teacher itself labels these uncertain. Independent ornithological claims about strict landing/feeding preferences and olive growth underwater were not established; no such claims printed. Noah's exact inferred interval before the first dove, speed of hundreds of exposed miles daily, and the raven's never landing on the ark are not made certain where the wording does not supply them.

## Genesis 8:13–19

- Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). Multiplication echoes the creation blessings; Romans 8 distinguishes preservation now from the final freedom of creation.
- Position source: none. Exact wording.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: exact total days converted to a particular solar year, the date of the last communication from God, and claims about all lifespans following a near-perfect genetic-error exponential are not established by the checked texts or independent scientific evidence. No source establishes that modern medicine merely masks a necessary present decline; no health/demographic claims used. What Noah observes versus God's later command is kept as the narrative gives it.

## Genesis 8:20–22

- Plan section: included, because (c). Pleasing sacrifice and a still-sinful humanity lead to the larger Christ-offering theme in Ephesians 5/Hebrews 10. Neither text is said to directly explain Noah's altar.
- Position source: none. Exact wording; existing Worship source has no developed interpretation of this unit.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Eden as a visibly occupied temple until the flood and a change to altars because that divine glory location was destroyed are not established by Genesis; excluded. No numerical proof of the exact count of sacrificed animals. The promise is not made a perpetual guarantee for the physical earth or a claim against all local disasters. Noah's altar is not said to cure human sin or settle a particular atonement mechanism. God's acceptance is stated without inventing a human bargaining cause behind the covenant.

## Genesis 9:1–17

- Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Isaiah 54 explicitly recalls Noah's promise; 2 Peter 3 relates flood judgment to God's patience and future righteousness. The larger covenant-faithfulness theme does not impose a dispensational system.
- Position source: none. No owner-authorized capital-punishment, dietary-law, or covenant-system conclusion is supplied by the site's studies. Exact wording.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: suzerainty defined as invariably unconditional and parity as the only other category are not established by the checked lexical entry or independent treaty-history source; no treaty taxonomy asserted. No claim that all world slaughterhouses always conform, only cults consume blood, or domestic animals are biologically exempt from all fear. No medical/blood-transfusion application. Theoretical animal-fear mechanisms, exact nutritional necessity, and all animal death as an assigned eucharistic metaphor are not independently established; excluded. First physical rainbow/no rain until flood remains unproved; Wesley's existing-rainbow suggestion is attributed rather than settled. The PDF's requirement of a death sentence for every instance is not turned into site policy; church differences are sourced from actual documents and current Catholic 2267. Civil-authority paragraphs are not overstated beyond their actual wording.

## Genesis 9:18–25

- Plan section: included, because (c). External preservation does not remove sin; promised inward renewal in Ezekiel 36 and new creation/reconciliation in 2 Corinthians 5 add a larger purpose beyond the family-disrespect lesson.
- Position source: none. Exact wording; no authorized conclusion on all details of Ham's act/curse.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Satan deceiving Noah, the exact emotional motives/celebration inside Ham, comparison assigning the roles of Adam/Eve/Satan to each family member, and precise ages/appearance of grandchildren are not stated in the checked narrative. No speculative sexual/castration reconstruction adopted; checked Wesley/USCCB notes focus on dishonor and do not establish those extra events. The baseline's claim that a curse must mean eternal damnation, all eight must be inwardly saved, God therefore could not curse Ham, and arbitrarily withholding grace from Canaan is the necessary solution is not established by Genesis/2 Peter or the earlier BDB curse check; excluded. The number-four/earth proof, modern ancestry/wealth maps, and unsupported genetic explanations are not used. The text names Canaan without stating a skin color or a curse upon all Ham's descendants; those actual limits govern the public explanation. Later Canaanite history is presented as Wesley's biblical reading, not independently dated archaeology.

## Genesis 9:26–29

- Plan section: included, because (a)/(b). Luke 3 directly places Shem/Noah in Jesus' ancestry; Ephesians 2's explicit shared household supports a labeled welcome comparison with tents.
- Position source: none. Exact wording; no owner-provided interpretation of these prophecies.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: mapping Shem/Japheth/Ham into fixed spiritual categories of every Jew, Gentile believer, or unbeliever is not established by Luke/Ephesians and is not asserted. Modern ethnic groups' roles as foils, continental wealth, exact later kingdoms or dates, and a full millennial economic program are not established by the checked sources. Precise genealogical overlap calculations, direct three-person transmission of all history, and six-thousand-year dates are not needed or claimed. Changing BSB youngest to younger based solely on the listing order is not established; BSB is retained. God dwelling versus Japheth dwelling is not decided through an unsupported lexical assertion; the brief prose does not over-specify the pronoun.
