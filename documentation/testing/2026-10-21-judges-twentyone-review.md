## Judges 21:1–25

Plan section: included, because (c). The book's unresolved need is followed by Ruth's explicit setting in the judges' period and the Davidic genealogy, which Matthew extends to Jesus and His mission to save from sins. Not a claim Ruth happens chronologically after the civil war or that each atrocity predicts a detail of Christ.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The oath and the elders' reasoning are narrated, not newly issued divine commands. No rule that every oath must be fulfilled even through crime, no automatic execution requirement for every broken vow, and no newly originated position on all lawful oaths.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The people weep, and 21:15 itself says the LORD made a void in the tribes. Do not present that narrator statement merely as an unfounded accusation the people make against God. Human acts and divine judgment are both present; no need to erase either.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Wesley regards the initial war as just and necessary while condemning its excess, unlike the baseline's broader condemnation. His own position is accurately described without making it a site verdict.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: USCCB notes and Gill both explain the giving/taking oath reasoning; this is not consent by the women, nor divine endorsement of abduction. No invented exact ages, festival identity, dowry negotiations, universal ban on mixed dancing, or claim the dancers caused their capture.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No 4/400 fallen-earth, 2/200 division, 6/600 sinful-man code. No claim these quantities prove God arranged exactly that number as a hidden commentary.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No ethnic stereotype about Jewish skill in evading rules, no certain picture of demons celebrating, no one-size judgment of all descendants. No claim the women are illegitimate or childbearing requires the approving of the method by which they were taken.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Jacob fearing for Benjamin is not here called an explicitly fulfilled prophecy of this civil war. No guarantee every human king remedies sin; the plan section rests on the actually read genealogies and Matthew's stated mission.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No independent historical detail retained without verification; no owner study edited or owner position originated.
