## Exodus 34:10–35

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Paul directly develops altar participation/idolatry, Christ the Passover lamb, resurrection firstfruits, and especially Moses' face/veil in 2Cor3. Each connection is explained within its own scope; no festival timetable imposed.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: God's renewal is announced in 34:10 and confirmed in 27; no claim formal renewal must wait forty years until Deuteronomy. Verse28's writer is read alongside God's explicit promise in34:1 and Deut10:4; no invented pen/chisel mechanics. The second stay explicitly lacks food and water; no manna explanation. No physical science explanation for radiance or guarantee fasting produces it.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Redeem sons means no human firstborn sacrifice. Sabbath, feasts, sacrifices and agricultural protection remain in their original Israel covenant setting; no modern guarantee that attending church prevents all loss. Threat of religious entanglement is not rewritten as a biological rule about ethnic purity. No generalized command for modern Christians to destroy other religions' property. The public teaching stays with the passage and Paul's warning to flee idolatry.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The text says Moses put on the veil after speaking; no silently reversed order. Paul supplies the fading-glory explanation; Exodus itself reports fear and the regular pattern. Do not present every Jew as eternally incapable of faith. Paul states the veil is removed when anyone turns to the Lord, and the Spirit transforms God's people. Not every calendar detail is a hidden end-times prediction. God's old covenant had real glory; it is not described as wholly worthless or evil.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No unverified species/cult origin for goat-milk rule, firstborn history, perpetual glow, or etymology of Deuteronomy. No entire Levite tribe equated with Aaronic priests or automatic claim Numbers3 explicitly makes the calf the sole reason for every priestly appointment. These details are unnecessary to the verified connections and omitted.
