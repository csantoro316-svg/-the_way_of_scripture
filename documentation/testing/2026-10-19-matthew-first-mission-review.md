## Per-group decisions

### Matthew10:1–4 — The Twelve Are Called and Sent

- Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Matthew19:27–30 explicitly links twelve thrones/tribes with renewal; Ephesians2:19–22 adds the gathered household and apostolic foundation with Christ the cornerstone. No arbitrary secret number code or modern organizational chart.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: no exact rank ladder derived from list order, no three lieutenants under Peter, no universal appointment of successors asserted or denied by a four-verse list. Acts1 actually has eleven before Matthias is added; no claim four lists all show the same twelve without qualification. John1 places Cephas/Peter naming with Jesus, so the baseline's Simon/Cephas/Peter all meaning rock and rock-name assigned at birth is not copied. No Greek/Hebrew name etymology beyond the actually read text. No Philip-the-apostle/Acts8-evangelist conflation, Bartholomew/Nathanael certainty, James the Less identification, exact age/birthplace/ranking, youngest John, or four Gospel writers all apostles. Later travel, martyrdom, boiling oil, burial sites, Andrew's cross/relics, Nero chronology, and author/canon dates are not independently checked and omitted. No proof Revelation necessarily last composed, every NT book's exact apostolic source, or no written Christian Scripture for a fixed number of decades. No claim all apostles personally appointed in undocumented private appearances, all current apostle claims false, all miraculous powers immune to imitation, or a new cessationist/continuationist decision. No Catholic omnipotence caricature; official primary documents checked. No claim every ministry impression is an irresistible command, all twelve untrained/below average, or list humility proves historicity. Judas' inclusion is explained without promising every named man's final fidelity or saving status. The plan now describes the collective apostolic calling rather than making a personal future-faithfulness promise about Judas.

### Matthew10:5–8 — Sent First to the Lost Sheep of Israel

- Plan section: included, because (c). Romans15:8–12 connects confirmation of Israel's promises with Gentile praise for mercy;1Peter4:10–11 adds grace entrusted for service and God's glory through Christ. Adds God's larger faithfulness and the gift's purpose beyond merely repeating the initial target/message.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: the baseline's promise/proposal/program/place four-stage system, Kingdom postponed solely because of Matthew12, Gentiles otherwise excluded from salvation, and every hypothetical alternative history are not established as consensus or site doctrine. No claim Spirit absent from all Gentiles before that point; reviewed Matthew8 already describes the centurion's faith. No universal belief every Jew automatically saved/no Gentile could enter, fixed three-population taxonomy, or Scripture never calling unbelieving Israel sheep. Lost-sheep language does not by itself settle regeneration before faith, fixed sheep/goat identities, all nonresponse proving permanent rejection, or one doctrine of election; those theological inferences require their own primary treatment. No assertive new view of water-and-Spirit, automatic new-birth sequence, or exact private work of the Spirit in each hearer. The Easter-egg/midwife/metal-detector illustrations are the teacher's own explanatory schemes, not the text's stated identities. No blanket rejection of reason, persuasion, polite discussion, or future response after initial resistance. “Preach” independently checked; no demand for rudeness or universal sales method. No universal identical understanding among every first-century Jew, denial of kingdom's present significance, guaranteed dates, or modern partisan/religious-group targeting. No present-day miracle guarantee or all gifts identical to the twelve's commission.1Peter's gift-service pattern is used without inventing an extra named gift or promising comparative results for particular people's prayers. Giving freely does not erase the next passage's support for workers or make every paid resource automatically sinful.

### Matthew10:9–15 — Depending on Welcome and Taking Rejection Seriously

- Plan section: included, because (c). Luke10:16 connects receiving messengers with receiving Jesus/the Father; Ephesians2:13–18 supplies peace through Christ's cross and shared access to the Father. Adds the saving source of the peace beyond hospitality logistics.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: no independently verified ancient road/footwashing/dust-shame universal practice; Mark6 explicitly calls the dust gesture testimony, and Acts13 records its use. Staff/sandal instructions differ in the actual accounts; Wesley's extra-preparation reconciliation is attributed rather than made the only reading. Later Luke22 purse/bag instructions and1Cor9 support prevent a manufactured universal packing regulation or salary ban. No imperative mood is erased to force an indicative automatic conversion from a peace greeting. “Worthy” as receptive/suitable is Wesley's reading and the immediate reception context, not proof of moral perfection or private predestination known to the messenger. No universal psychology-of-reciprocity mechanism, manipulative obligation, gift as forced sales transaction, or claim every accident is a prearranged encounter with a detectable elect person. No private guilt rule tells everyone to stop giving or serving. No Spirit-as-metal-detector formula for identifying permanent goats, no claim any brief positive reply guarantees regeneration, and no private feeling authorizes abandoning all future witness. No comparative miracle frequency caused by preparation, no instruction to seek avoidable danger, and no judgment on a named modern missionary's martyr status from news not independently checked. The later lesson itself recognizes preparation; no blanket rejection of thinking/planning. No equation of peace greeting with an entire gospel presentation or of lodging with a complete mandatory discipleship plan. Rejection is serious; apostles are told to leave, not force reception or punish households. No newly invented final-destiny ruling on a person who declines one conversation, no full judgment timetable or claim the comparison applies only to one national generation. Broad covenant/kingdom systems are not imposed on the warnings.
