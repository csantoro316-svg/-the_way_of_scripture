## Titus 3:1–8

Plan section: included, because (a)/(b)/(c). The text explicitly joins God's mercy, new birth, the Spirit poured out through Christ and inheritance of eternal life. Ezekiel 36:25–27 gives the cleansing/new-heart/Spirit parallel; Acts 2:14–18, 32–33 explicitly locates the Spirit's promised outpouring in the risen, exalted Christ.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The baseline's perfect-tense claim for remind and its precise ancient authority usage are not independently verified or included. No formal Greek tense lesson, ordinary conjunction always-greater maxim or rule that an English infinitive is itself a Greek imperative is created. The commands' actual substance is explained without unverified grammar. No contemporary political endorsement, legal advice or automatic equation of every official's command with God's moral will is supplied; Wesley's own conscience limitation is accurately named.

The baseline's unconditional election/order-of-salvation scheme is not inserted as the only common interpretation or God's-plan framework. Its declaration that no possible human faith response matters is not made universal consensus. The actual text's mercy rather than prior righteous deeds is retained with its call to good works afterward. No film quotation, cartoon scene, modern sociological inference or psychological story about every convert's precise former conduct is copied.

The baseline categorically excludes baptism from the washing phrase and treats the baptismal reading as mainly Catholic. Actual Thayer, Wesley, Calvin, Lutheran Small Catechism and USCCB all document a baptism connection. Catholic 1215 and Lutheran baptism teaching affirm new birth as God's gift through water and word; Westminster 28 distinguishes effective Spirit-given grace from universal automatic regeneration and a fixed administrative moment; Baptist 1689 29 describes a sign for professing believers. These own documents are read directly and represented accurately. No opinion that water works by itself, that all Protestants share a purely external reading or that a tradition teaches people can buy salvation is invented. No new owner baptismal-regeneration or infant-baptism verdict is originated.

The baseline's Nicodemus biography and categorical interpretation of every water reference are not independently reconstructed. No causal assertion that every past calamity was specific divine discipline or every questioner is spiritually dead is supplied. The plan connections are Scripture-supported parallels rather than a complete imposed denominational account of how grace and response work.

## Titus 3:9–11

Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). First Corinthians 1:10–13 grounds unity against rival followings in the undivided Christ; Ephesians 4:1–6 joins humility, patience and unity in the Spirit. The aim is care for that shared life beyond winning a private dispute.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The baseline's first lesson calls Simon a believer, while the following lesson concludes he never truly believed. Neither is adopted as a verified final biography from Acts 8. Its speculation about his emotional state, having no possibility of repentance and never receiving any inward conviction is not public fact. The existing independently researched Acts passage is not rewritten as part of this focused batch.

Thayer supports factious/divisive conduct; the public text explains that sense and the actual warning sequence. It does not create an exclusive dictionary definition for every historical use of heretic, infer every disagreement proves unbelief or give a new owner church-discipline procedure. Calvin expressly distinguishes sincere differences from obstinate disruption and requires warning first; Wesley explains persistent faction-making. Calvin's allowance for separate magistrate action is not disguised as shared noncoercion doctrine. No physical punishment, civil censorship, irredeemable psychological diagnosis or rule forbidding all later repentance is introduced.

The baseline's stand-around-surrounding-with-disapproval picture for avoid was not independently verified and is omitted. No gangrene clinical staging or modern hospital graphic is required to explain this paragraph. No certain identity of every Alexander across letters, detailed intention of every warned person, or necessary conclusion about final salvation is invented. Warning and boundary remain linked to actual continuing destructive behavior rather than merely a question.

## Titus 3:12–15

Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). Philippians 4:14–20 directly connects practical provision with gospel partnership and fruit; Ephesians 4:28 links honest work and sharing with need. This gives the wider mission and use-of-resources purpose beyond the travel details.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The exact sending of Artemas rather than Tychicus is not established by mentioning a separate letter's sending of Tychicus to Ephesus; the chronology is not certain from this paragraph. The text gives alternatives. No complete biography, age, observed training course or home assignment of Artemas is invented. The particular Nicopolis, port geography, ancient seasonal travel restrictions, etymology, letter-writing date and precise westward church-planting plan were not independently researched and are unnecessary public context. The text's own intended winter stay and invitation are retained.

Zenas is called a lawyer; his exact field is not certain, and neither a Roman license nor a specific Jewish scholarly rank is assumed. Paul asks for practical provision without specifying a fixed collection or budget. Calvin and Wesley's actual support/help explanations are named. The baseline's local volunteer needs, spiritual-gift test policy, classroom anecdote and current institution's internal practices are not made universal commands for the site.

The final greeting does not explicitly declare hatred, lack of love or an intended kick toward every unnamed opponent. Its stated love-in-faith and grace-for-all wording is retained without inventing a hostile motive. No exact outcome for the visitors, final location of every companion or fulfilled travel report is supplied. The plan's practical partnership does not turn good work into the cause that earns the saving mercy described earlier.
