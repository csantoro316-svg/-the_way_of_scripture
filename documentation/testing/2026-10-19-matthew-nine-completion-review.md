## Per-group decisions

### Matthew9:18–26 — A Daughter Restored and a Woman Healed

- Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). John5:21–29 explicitly describes the Son giving life and the dead hearing His voice;1Corinthians15:42–55 supplies the lasting resurrection beyond this girl's return to ordinary life. The episode is a glimpse/picture alongside that explicit promise, not a detail-by-detail allegory.
- Position source: Prayer and Faith, section5; limited to the woman's faith in verses20–22.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: blanket no-Jew-ever-handled-coins/no-bowing-to-humans claims, automatic death penalty for kneeling, Jairus' exact personal risks, all synagogue administrative customs, and his private motives are not independently verified. The earlier proskuneō lexicon already contradicts treating all bowing as exclusively deity-worship. Mark/Luke name Jairus; no speculative tribunal or exact house route is added. Matthew presents death in the initial plea, while Mark/Luke narrate later news: the differences are explained without a fabricated messenger reaching him before Matthew's recorded plea. The father's supposed three private tests, impatience, memory of Malachi, suppressed mourning, and exactly what he learned from the woman are not stated. No claim grief itself means failing a faith test, genuine mourners cannot laugh, or every person was a paid performer. Actual flute/noisy crowd details suffice; ancient professional mourner practices and required numbers lack independent checks. Luke explicitly confirms death/restoration; no medical misdiagnosis is inferred. No claim death is literally unconscious sleep or that unbelievers' deaths are never called sleep. No assertion Jesus literally absorbed ritual uncleanness as a curse, that every touching transferred all guilt, or that the girl earned life through her own conscious faith. Numbers12 being always God's governmental code is unproved. The proposed woman/justification, father/sanctification, girl/glorification three-stage allegory and feeding-first/Kingdom-banquet timetable lack a scriptural interpretation of these details and are not published. Both12-year details may be observed but do not compel that scheme. No special future resurrection schedule, all grief reduced to temporary separation with no loss, or tragedies only tragic when lessons missed. Present compassion and real grief are not erased by future hope.

### Matthew9:20–22 — Faith Reaches Out to Jesus

- Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). Mark5:34's personal peace is placed alongside Romans5:1–2's peace/access through Christ; Titus3:3–7 adds saving mercy/renewal/future hope. Labeled as a picture beside the wider teaching, not a claim a healing verb proves every salvation conclusion.
- Position source: Prayer and Faith, section5, directly developed Matthew9:20–22 discussion.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: no diagnosis, menstrual duration calculation, known home relocation, deception, estranged relatives, complete total exclusion from all social life, or uniform rabbinic refusal to speak to women is independently established. Leviticus15's actual prolonged-discharge/cleansing instructions are cited without making illness a moral failing or reading every associated touch as the same legal category. The text does not give her childhood schooling, exact Malachi memory, secret motive, or all private thoughts beyond her recorded saying. Malachi4:1–3 was directly checked as the baseline's proposed connection; it pictures healing in the sun's wings, but Matthew does not identify this woman's garment-touch as its fulfillment. No actual kanaph lexicon entry was read in this batch, so no unverified lexical proof or hidden tassel prophecy is published. Blue cord is stated in Numbers; later five-knot/royal/priestly symbolism, tassel signatures on tablets, divorcing by cutting hems, and Pharisaic clothing competitions lack independent sources. No claim Jesus was unaware of all events, not the healer, surprised into helplessness, or only another divine person acted; Mark/Luke narrate His awareness/question without resolving all Christological questions. Public confession of deceit is not what the text says: she tells what happened. No magic clothing or impersonal power withdrawal mechanism. The12-number and salvation-stages flags above also apply. The old LSB quote and study's inexact BSB text are replaced rather than relabeled.

### Matthew9:35–38 — Compassion and Workers for the Harvest

- Plan section: included, because (b)/(c). Numbers27:15–23 supplies the sheep/leader/prayer parallel; Ezekiel34:11–16,23–24 supplies God's promised gathering and Davidic shepherd; John10:11–18 adds the good shepherd's death/resurrection and one flock. Connects workers' compassionate service with Christ's saving work beyond a staffing appeal.
- Position source: none.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: exact240-town count, length of ministry, universal crowd disappointment, every person's Messiah verdict, and all leaders' corrupt motives are not independently established. The BSB says harassed/helpless, not necessarily only worried because all rejected Jesus. Actual Thayer usages checked. No zoological claims that sheep are universally dim-witted, cannot be driven, follow shadows, or must behave in one way. No divinely guaranteed dissatisfaction feeling that always identifies true/false teaching, unquestioning obedience rule, or broad dismissal of all nonbiblical learning. No new timetable of a rejected Kingdom, national replacement system, or claim Israel had never heard of seeking others before. The immediate sending in Matthew10 is to Israel, and no unrecorded all-nations commission is moved into this moment. The harvest is God's and workers are sent; not every detail is assigned a fixed secret referent or limited to one professional clergy office. Prayer and willingness to serve are joined without claiming every petitioner must become a pastor or one emotional impression proves a specific calling. No site position invented from the prayer topic alone.
