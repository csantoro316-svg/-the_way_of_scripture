## Hebrews 5:1–4

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Hebrews 7:26–28 and 9:11–14 directly develop the older priest comparison in Christ's sinless once-for-all offering and purified service.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Priesthood comparison does not make Christ sinful; the priests' own-sin offerings are distinguished. No modern surname or universal lineage proof, political eligibility rule or legal-licensing analogy needed. Those claims were not independently checked and are excluded. Sacrificial worship regulations do not establish that no Israelite could ever pray, thank, sing, or receive mercy away from a priest. The text's specific priestly work is retained without a universal ban on any other form of devotion.

No automatic import of all Aaronic high-priest qualifications into every Christian leadership office; no owner doctrine of ordination originated here. Dealing gently is not permission to excuse wrongdoing or a guarantee every historical priest displayed gentleness. Calling by God remains central, without inventing an appointment process the passage does not give.

## Hebrews 5:5–10

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Romans 5:18–19 and Philippians 2:6–11 explicitly join obedience with saving work and exaltation; Hebrews 7:24–25 develops the living permanent intercessor.
Position source: Who Shepherds the Church? — lines 44–63.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Baseline says order necessarily means ancestral succession, correct family line, previous priests from whom Christ inherited. Direct Thayer/BDB and Hebrews 7:15–17 do not support that claim and the latter expressly opposes physical succession as ground of Christ's office. No invented genealogy. Its assertion that the Father first became Father of the Son only at human birth is not silently imposed on traditions affirming eternal Sonship. Public describes divine appointment from the quotations without resolving every today interpretation anew.

Jesus' earthly prayers/tears and being heard do not imply avoiding death. Calvin/Wesley have differing nuances of heard/from fear versus reverence; BSB because of reverence main quote retained. Baseline identifies John 17 as in Gethsemane, but John 18:1 places the crossing into the garden after that prayer; do not assert the location. No speculative sequence of every tear or thought, or claim that prior to incarnation disobedience first became a real possibility within God. Real human obedience and sinlessness are affirmed without origination of a theory about divine potential to sin.

Learning/completion do not imply past moral defect or divine ignorance; obedience also is not redefined so that no faithful response matters. Calvin includes reception in faith and imitation, Wesley obedience doing/suffering God's will; no new owner faith/works system. John 19:30 uses a related but different verb from teleioo; do not claim identical Greek lemmas merely because English meanings overlap. Christ's completed offering does not mean every remaining event in God's plan already occurred.

## Hebrews 5:11–14

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Ephesians 4:11–16 explicitly describes body maturation/protection from false teaching; Romans 12:1–2 joins renewed mind, discernment and faithful living.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: No need to master jargon such as soteriology or eschatology before being considered a mature Christian; the practical discernment goal is explained plainly. The text does not diagnose disability, low intelligence, every child's nutritional condition, or prove these churches were exactly a decade old. No fixed study/prayer/worship/service/fellowship priority ladder prescribed by the passage. No rule everyone must become an ordained teacher or should cease all basics.

Hard to explain is not lexically guaranteed to mean to misjudge an interpretation; the teacher's confident etymology was not verified and is excluded. No identity claimed for we as a known apostles' committee or Holy Spirit coauthor group. Discernment through practice includes understanding/lived use, without a guarantee that acquiring facts automatically prevents every error or that today's understanding transfers unchanged as a fixed eternal maturity rank. The latter is the baseline's speculative next-chapter conclusion, not a conclusion of 5:11–14.
