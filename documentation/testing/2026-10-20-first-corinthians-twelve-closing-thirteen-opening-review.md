## 1 Corinthians 12:27–31

Plan section: included, because (a): Ephesians 2:19–22 explicitly places apostles and prophets within God’s household built together under Christ the cornerstone.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Paul’s first/second/third order is retained without creating a complete numerical ranking of every gift or claiming teachers are now always the highest gift. The baseline’s cessation/closed-canon inference is not made a site position. Distinct value of a task does not make any person disposable. The plurality of the addressees does not by itself prove individuals may never pray for gifts; the baseline’s blanket prohibition is not adopted. Claims about every Corinthian’s motives, everyone speaking simultaneously and all their gift-seeking methods are not invented history. The rhetorical questions and AG’s initial-sign distinction are both represented; no claim Pentecostals simply ignore the text. No advice to reject healing or care in favor of attending a lesson is imported. Calvin’s church-edification reading is attributed without adopting his combative rhetoric or every later footnote’s office identifications.

## 1 Corinthians 13:1–3

Plan section: included, because (a): John 13:34–35 explicitly makes Christ’s own love the pattern and identifying sign of His disciples.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Main BSB verse 3 differs from the baseline’s burned wording; actual text and footnote were checked and distinguished. Tongues of angels is not conclusively ruled impossible or established as the language of every contemporary claim. Calvin’s deliberately heightened reading and AG’s possibility of heavenly speech are attributed fairly. No actual angel vocabulary or phonetics is inferred. No exhaustive gift-of-martyrdom category is invented from the example. Paul’s nothing language is not restricted to a precise reward formula or expanded into a new owner salvation doctrine. Giving’s visible cost does not independently establish motive or replace love.

## 1 Corinthians 13:4–7

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c): Ephesians 5:1–2 explicitly connects imitation of God and walking in love with Christ’s self-giving sacrifice.
Position source: none.
FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Agape is not asserted to lexically mean only divine, unconditional self-sacrifice; the actually read Thayer opening gives a wider range. Calvin’s printed lexical spelling in the inspected verse 4 excerpt was not independently verified and is not reproduced as Greek analysis. Love is not reduced to sentiment, silence about wrongdoing, credulity or denying harm. Paul’s rejoicing with truth and chapter 5 correction remain relevant context. The baseline’s literal forgetting of every offense and its exact added phrase for Christ’s sake after all four clauses are not treated as wording in the text. Endurance does not require declaring a lie true or praising evil. No claim one list exhausts every possible expression of love is made. The church setting is retained without limiting these qualities to congregational meetings or only romantic relationships.
