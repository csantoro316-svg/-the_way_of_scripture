## Matthew 27:51–54

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Hebrews 9–10 directly develops priestly access through Christ; 1 Peter 2 and Romans 12 describe worship/service; 1 Corinthians 15 grounds resurrection hope in Christ.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The PDF insists the outer veil tore and denies access to the Father if it were inner. Josephus establishes two curtains but not which tore; actual Thayer explicitly identifies the Gospel referent as inner. Hebrews 10:19–22 explicitly invites approach through Christ while retaining His priesthood, so inner-curtain access does not remove the need for Him. Unsupported exclusive outer-curtain claim omitted, alongside 60ft/30ft/four-inch/3,100lb claims and imagined weeks of crowds on the Mount of Olives. No definite 6pm time independent of season/local conditions. No fixed Thursday AD27 death date or two distinct consecutive Sabbaths established by these verses. No unsupported claim of a worldwide earthquake, falling structures, exact sunlight/finished/earthquake timing, or earthquake mechanism. Matthew places appearances after Jesus' resurrection without specifying every stage's timing; no invented three days sitting in tombs. Saints' names, bodies' eventual fate and later second deaths are not supplied. Calvin allows uncertainty and prefers ongoing life, unlike the baseline's certainty they died again; public wording leaves fate unstated. Lazarus was four days dead in John 11, not the PDF's three, and that comparison's chronology is not used. No guarantee of every soldier's lifelong conversion from Luke 23:34: Calvin explicitly differs. No absolute claim unbelievers cannot address God in prayer, no literal one-to-one furniture assignments as Matthew's meaning, no new church ministry policy from the curtain. The directly supported service connections are retained in their own scriptural terms.

## Matthew 27:55–56

Plan section: omitted, because none apply beyond the context and interpretation already explained. Their continued witness is covered there; no filler section.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The PDF's shorthand three Marys misstates Matthew's actual list; Jesus' mother is named in John, not substituted for the mother of Zebedee's sons in Matthew. No exact identity of every similarly named James/Joseph, no height explanation for James the Less, no claim all male followers had vanished (Luke 23:49 and John 19:25–27 qualify that), no assertion women were physically incapable of burial work, no cultural verdict that only women remaining was shameful. No invented appeals for men to help, timeline of every movement, or claim these women witnessed every minute without interruption. Their actual repeated presence is enough.

## Matthew 27:57–61

Plan section: included, because (a)/(b). Burial belongs to the gospel in 1 Corinthians 15 and Acts 13. Isaiah 53:9 is a labeled scriptural parallel, not an explicit Matthew quotation. Jesus in Joseph's own tomb can illustrate substitution in light of Romans 5, labeled reflection, not a newly declared code in the text.

Position source: none. The Gospel and I Am a Disciple links provide related study, not authority to invent an unworked burial position.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: The PDF claims no burial spices were available or used; John 19:39–40 explicitly says Nicodemus brought spices and they wrapped Jesus with them. Public text follows John. Luke says Joseph did not consent to the council's action, not that Joseph and Nicodemus certainly both missed the trial. No identification of Joseph as Pharisee, exact Arimathea location/distance, financial cost in today's currency, expulsion/lost career details, or crowds of pleading women is independently established. No private-motive speeches or Pilate's unrecorded reasoning. No assertion only a Council member could secure permission. No inevitable seven-day burial impossibility or fixed three-hour count. No unsupported first-century burial/ossuary timeline or home-storage generalization. Exodus 34:25/Passover leftovers may invite reflection but does not explicitly require Messiah buried before sunset, so that mechanism is omitted. Joseph getting his tomb back is a possible observation, not a stated later transaction; not published as history.

## Matthew 27:62–66

Plan section: included, because (a)/(c). Matthew 16:21's promise and Acts 13's account of condemnation, burial, fulfilled promises and resurrection carry the story beyond precautions.

Position source: none.

FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Guard's Roman versus temple identity is not settled merely by Pilate's words; USCCB explicitly explains the ambiguity. No invented four/sixteen-man unit, mandatory execution penalty, seal material or exclusive ownership. No labeling Sadducees a modern political wing, no claim their denial of resurrection means they positively taught everlasting bodiless existence, no speculative majority-vote motive. No asserted chronology that plural Sabbath usage requires Thursday death, fixed April10AD27 date, exact astronomical tables, or 2027 anniversary prediction. Later Matthew describes officials paying guards; do not read the later bribe into this earlier request. Guard account is not made a claim every conceivable historical objection is logically impossible.
