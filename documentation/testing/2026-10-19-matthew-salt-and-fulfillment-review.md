## Per-group decisions

### Matthew5:13–16 — Salt and Light

- Plan section: included, because(b)/(c). Isaiah49:6 light/nations applied to apostolic mission in Acts13:46–48, closely corresponding good-deeds/glory/light pattern in1Peter2:9–12, and Ephesians2:8–10 grace producing a visible new life. These add wider mission/rescue context beyond simply repeating the analogies.
- Position source: The Gospel According to Jesus — The Fullness, Sermon as character of King and grace-shaped life.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: exclusivity of preserving rather than flavor as Jesus' single intended meaning, exactly impure salt for food preservation/pure salt only table use, price/availability/mining process, particular deposits losing salt after rain, and universal Galilean audience assumptions were not independently verified. Cato establishes preservation generally, not those details. No salt-chemistry reconstruction presented as necessary meaning of the verse. Every failing Christian being literally trampled or worldwide Gentile presence mathematically preventing total decay is not established. The PDF's exact lamp cost, only lighting when essential, every household's oil/fire practices and a blanket instead of the BSB basket are not assumed. Good deeds are not reduced to hidden inward traits so concrete care is dismissed. The PDF's classification of SalvationArmy alongside secular substitutes was not checked against its own documents and is not used as an argument about the church. No unsupported generalization every outsider lacks mercy or every act of charity without a stated religious label is worthless. Current ritual/clothing/community stereotypes are excluded. Jesus explicitly assigns glory to the Father; no new site theory of salt as a guaranteed social/political program or of final salvation loss originated.

### Matthew5:17–20 — Fulfilling the Law and the Prophets

- Plan section: included, because(a)/(c). Jesus explicitly explains Scripture's fulfillment around suffering/rising/proclamation in Luke24; Hebrews10 and Colossians2 show earlier shadows reaching Christ; Romans3/8 connect grace/righteousness/Spirit-enabled life. No imposed timetable or comprehensive law code settled by inference.
- Position source: Is the Bible the Inerrant Word of God? — Scripture's inspired authority and distinction original texts/later translations only.
- FLAGGED FOR REVIEW: Scripture's Law/Prophets wording cannot be replaced with all-human-rules or limited to a particular printed Mishnah. The PDF's claim completed written Mishnah/six divisions/1000-page book universally controlled first-century life is an anachronistic reconstruction not independently verified; not published. Exact Ezra-school origin, ten extra rules per command, oral/written collection dates, every Sabbath charge necessarily only rabbinic, and one single motive explaining all Pharisee hostility are unsupported. No blanket claim all Pharisees/Mormons/Catholics only read their secondary book or have identical morals. Kid boiled in mother's milk as a uniquely Baal ritual, exact butcher/milk scenario creating dairy-meat separation, Canaanite extinction date, kosher refrigerator practice and comparative modern Jewish sexual morality were not verified from independent primary sources; none used. Hebrew letter/stroke explanation checked in actual lexicons, without turning every modern English punctuation mark into a divinely fixed autograph. Modern manuscript theories and inerrancy details not all proved by one sentence. Least/great wording is reported; baseline's certainty that every violator remains saved with lower rank is not certified by checked Gill/Wesley (both discuss exclusion); no new owner conclusion about final rank/exclusion or verse20's whole salvation mechanism. Nor is the verse treated as proving every Mosaic rite eternally unchanged; checked primary confessions distinguish ritual and moral aspects. Exact application of each law requires its own study. Grace/faith and renewed obedience supported by actual Romans texts, not a new moral-achievement formula. Jesus' words do not authorize taking every present-day religious teacher as universally faithless.
